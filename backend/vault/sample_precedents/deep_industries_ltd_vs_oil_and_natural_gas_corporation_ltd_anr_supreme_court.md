# Deep Industries Ltd Vs. Oil And Natural Gas Corporation Ltd Anr
* **Citation:** (2019) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2019-11-27
* **Parties:** Justice V. Ramasubramanian Judgment

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 —Sections 5 , 16 , 17 , 29A , 34 , 37 — Article 227 of the Constitution of India —Scope of High Court’s supervisory jurisdiction under Article 227 over orders passed in appeals under Section 37 of the Act—Held, while Article 227 jurisdiction is not ousted by Section 5, the High Court must exercise extreme circumspection and restrict interference to cases of patent lack of inherent jurisdiction, in light of the statutory policy of minimal judicial intervention and speedy disposal under the Act—High Court’s interference on merits and inversion of the statutory scheme is impermissible—Impugned High Court order set aside; arbitration proceedings to continue expeditiously.
