# Sunil Kumar Jain And Others Vs. Sundaresh Bhatt And Others
* **Citation:** (2022) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2022-04-18
* **Parties:** and in the case of Gujarat Urja Vikas Nigam Limited v. Amit Gupta, Civil Appeal No

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 —Sections 5(13) , 20 , 36(4) , 53 —Claims of workmen/employees for wages/salaries during CIRP and prior period—Supreme Court holds that only wages/salaries of those workmen/employees who actually worked when the corporate debtor was a going concern during CIRP are to be included in CIRP costs and paid in priority as per Section 53(1)(a); other wage/salary dues to be governed by Section 53(1)(b) and (c)—Amounts due towards provident fund, gratuity, and pension fund are excluded from liquidation estate under Section 36(4) and must be paid to workmen/employees from such funds, with no claim by the liquidator—Disputed claims to be adjudicated by the liquidator on evidence— Appeal partly allowed .
