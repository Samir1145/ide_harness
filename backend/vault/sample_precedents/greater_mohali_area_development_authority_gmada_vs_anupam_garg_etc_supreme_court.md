# Greater Mohali Area Development Authority Gmada Vs. Anupam Garg Etc
* **Citation:** N/A
* **Court:** N/A
* **Date:** N/A
* **Parties:** N/A

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Consumer Protection Act, 1986 – Sections relating to deficiency in service and compensation – Greater Mohali Area Development Authority (GMADA) challenged orders directing refund of deposited amounts with 8% interest and additional compensation, including interest paid by allottees on home loans – Supreme Court held that while compensation for delay and mental agony is permissible, the authority cannot be saddled with liability to pay the interest incurred by allottees on their home loans in addition to contractual interest – Appeals allowed to the extent of setting aside the direction to pay interest on the loans, but other compensation and refund orders upheld.
