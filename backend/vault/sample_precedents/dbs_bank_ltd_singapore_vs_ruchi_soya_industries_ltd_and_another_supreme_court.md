# Dbs Bank Ltd Singapore Vs. Ruchi Soya Industries Ltd And Another
* **Citation:** (2024) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2024-01-02
* **Parties:** Singapore v. Ruchi Soya Industries Ltd

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 —Section 30(2)(b)(ii) (as amended in 2019)—Whether a dissenting financial creditor is entitled to be paid the minimum value of its security interest—Held, the amended Section 30(2)(b)(ii) applies to pending proceedings, including appeals, and mandates that dissenting financial creditors must receive at least the liquidation value of their security interest in monetary terms, not less than what would be payable under Section 53(1) of the Code—Dissenting financial creditors must forgo their security interest and cannot enforce it post-approval of the resolution plan—Conflicting views in India Resurgence ARC Pvt. Ltd. v. Amit Metaliks Ltd. distinguished; matter referred to a larger Bench for authoritative determination— Reference to Larger Bench directed .
