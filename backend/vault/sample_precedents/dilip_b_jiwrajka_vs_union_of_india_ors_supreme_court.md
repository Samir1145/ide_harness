# Dilip B Jiwrajka Vs. Union Of India Ors
* **Citation:** (2023) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2023-11-08
* **Parties:** Jiwrajka v. Union of India and Ors

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 — Sections 95 to 100 —Constitutional validity—Challenge to the process for initiation of insolvency resolution against individuals and personal guarantors—Supreme Court holds that the role of the Resolution Professional under Section 99 is purely facilitative and recommendatory, not adjudicatory; no judicial determination is required at the stage of appointment of the Resolution Professional under Section 97(5) ; principles of natural justice are satisfied by the opportunity for representation before the Resolution Professional and a full hearing before the Adjudicating Authority under Section 100 ; the interim-moratorium under Section 96 protects the debtor but does not freeze assets; the impugned provisions do not violate Articles 14 or 21 of the Constitution; writ petitions dismissed .
