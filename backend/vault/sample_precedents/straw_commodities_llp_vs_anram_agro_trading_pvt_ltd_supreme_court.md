# Straw Commodities Llp Vs. Anram Agro Trading Pvt Ltd
* **Citation:** (2025) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2025-03-06
* **Parties:** SC IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA Straw Commodities LLP v. Anram Agro Trading Pvt

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Refusal to interfere with rejection of insolvency proceedings does not bar independent money recovery suit on evidence Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 – proceedings under; Limitation Act, 1963 – condonation of delay – Insolvency proceedings – Rejection of proceedings under IBC – Effect on civil remedy – Appeal against impugned order of NCLAT – Supreme Court declining interference – liberty reserved to appellant to file suit for recovery of money within one month – trial court directed to consider condonation application, if any, in accordance with Limitation Act – observations in impugned order made for rejecting IBC proceedings not to affect adjudication of suit on basis of evidence on record – HELD : No ground made out to interfere with impugned order rejecting proceedings under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016; however, appellant granted liberty to institute suit for recovery within one month, with any delay application to be considered under the Limitation Act, 1963, and findings in the impugned order not to prejudice such suit. Civil appeal dismissed .
