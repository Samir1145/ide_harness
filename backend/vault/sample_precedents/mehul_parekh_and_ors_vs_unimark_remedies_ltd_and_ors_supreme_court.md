# Mehul Parekh And Ors Vs. Unimark Remedies Ltd And Ors
* **Citation:** (2024) SC
* **Court:** in the appeal against the order of the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal
* **Date:** 2024-02-15
* **Parties:** N/A

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote In a Civil Appeal under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 , the Supreme Court, comprising Dr. Justice Dhananjaya Y Chandrachud (CJI), Mr. Justice J.B. Pardiwala, and Mr. Justice Manoj Misra, held that no substantial question of law arises in the appeal against the order of the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal ( NCLAT ), and accordingly dismissed the appeal . The Court reaffirmed the direction for expeditious disposal of the pending proceedings by the National Company Law Tribunal ( NCLT ), specifically requesting early consideration of the Avoidance Application. Pending applications were also disposed of.

### Case Summary
Summary In a Civil Appeal under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 , the Supreme Court, comprising Dr. Justice Dhananjaya Y Chandrachud (CJI), Mr. Justice J.B. Pardiwala, and Mr. Justice Manoj Misra, held that no substantial question of law arises in the appeal against the order of the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal ( NCLAT ), and accordingly dismissed the appeal . The Court reaffirmed the direction for expeditious disposal of the pending proceedings by the National Company Law Tribunal ( NCLT ), specifically requesting early consideration of the Avoidance Application. Pending applications were also disposed of.
