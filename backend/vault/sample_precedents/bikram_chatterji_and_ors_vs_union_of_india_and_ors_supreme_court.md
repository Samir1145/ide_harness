# Bikram Chatterji And Ors Vs. Union Of India And Ors
* **Citation:** (2021) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2021-06-28
* **Parties:** Bikram Chatterji and others vs. Union of India and others

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Constitution of India – Article 142; Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 – Sections relating to registration and project completion; Orders dated 23.07.2019 and 14.10.2019 of the Supreme Court – Applications seeking recall or modification of directions regarding the status of “Amrapali La Residentia” project and surrender of 632 flats or 19.75% of project land. Held that the Company (La Residentia Developers Pvt. Ltd.) cannot be declared as part of the Amrapali Group of Companies, nor can the entire project or the component of 632 flats be handed over to NBCC for completion, as the project is at an advanced stage of construction and such transfer would escalate costs to the detriment of flat buyers. The directions in the orders dated 23.07.2019 and 14.10.2019 are not to be recalled or revisited. The Company shall continue with the construction and development of the project, and the 632 flats may be sold under the supervision of the Court Receiver, with sale proceeds (after deducting certified construction costs) credited for the benefit of Amrapali Group flat buyers. Applications seeking recall or modification are rejected ; directions issued for sale and accounting of 632 flats as per modified terms.
