# N Subramanian Vs. M S Aruna Hotels Ltd Anr
* **Citation:** (2021) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2020-03-02
* **Parties:** Subramanian v. Aruna Hotels Ltd

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Acknowledgement dated 30.09.2014 within three years of a Section 9 IBC application keeps arrears of salary for at least three years prior within limitation and negates “existence of dispute” Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 – Sections 8, 9, 14 – Operational debt – Arrears of salary – Limitation – Acknowledgement of liability – “Existence of dispute” – Application under Section 9 by erstwhile employee claiming arrears from 1998 to retirement in 2013 – Acknowledgements including letter dated 30.09.2014 appending exact amount due – NCLT admitted petition, appointed IRP and imposed moratorium under Section 14 – NCLAT set aside admission, relying inter alia on EPFO letter dated 13.04.2016 and unexplained delay since 1998 – HELD : From 30.09.2014 (last acknowledgement) to filing of petition on 27.07.2017, three years had not elapsed; therefore, at least arrears of salary for a period of three years prior to 30.09.2014 were within limitation and payable – EPFO letter was a red-herring and had nothing to do with arrears of salary – Acknowledgement of liability showed no “dispute” as to amounts owed – NCLAT judgment set aside and NCLT judgment restored ; appeal allowed .

### Case Summary
