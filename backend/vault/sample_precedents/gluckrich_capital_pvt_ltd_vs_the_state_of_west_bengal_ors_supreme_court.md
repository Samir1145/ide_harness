# Gluckrich Capital Pvt Ltd Vs. The State Of West Bengal Ors
* **Citation:** (2023) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2023-05-18
* **Parties:** s judgment, which, relying on Usha Ananthasubramanian v. Union of India 1 and has erroneously held that Section 66 of the IBC cannot be invoked against other persons, entities or organizations with which there was any business transaction by the corporate debtor, but only the persons who were responsible for the conduct of business of the corporate debtor can be proceeded against

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 – Section 66 – Application seeking clarification of Supreme Court order dismissing SLP for want of locus – Applicant, an unsecured financial creditor, sought clarification that the dismissal would not preclude the Resolution Professional from initiating proceedings under Section 66 of IBC against persons or entities allegedly involved in fraudulent transactions with the corporate debtor – The Supreme Court held that such clarification cannot be granted to indirectly challenge the Tripura High Court’s judgment, which, relying on Usha Ananthasubramanian v. Union of India (2020) 4 SCC 122, correctly held that Section 66 applies only to persons responsible for the conduct of the corporate debtor’s business and not to third-party organizations or entities – Remedy against third parties lies in independent civil action, not under Section 66 – Application for clarification dismissed.
