# Dyna Technologies Pvt Ltd Vs. Crompton Greaves Ltd
* **Citation:** (2019) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2019-12-17
* **Parties:** s Sundarsan Trading Company v. Government of Kerala

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 – Sections 31(3), 34, 34(4), 37 – Requirement of reasoned arbitral award – Scope of judicial interference – Held that the arbitral award must contain proper, intelligible, and adequate reasons as mandated by Section 31(3); an award lacking such reasoning is unintelligible and cannot be sustained. The High Court, upon finding the award devoid of reasons, ought to have considered remanding the matter to the Tribunal under Section 34(4) to cure the defect, but given the protracted litigation, the Supreme Court directed payment of Rs. 30,00,000/- in full and final settlement of the disputed claim. Appeal disposed of accordingly.
