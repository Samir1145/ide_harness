# China Development Bank Vs. Doha Bank Q P S C And Ors
* **Citation:** (2024) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2024-12-19
* **Parties:** SC IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA China Development Bank v. Doha Bank Q

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 — Sections 3(6), 3(11), 3(12), 5(7), 5(8)(i), 7(1), 14, 15(1) ; Indian Contract Act, 1872 — Sections 126, 127, 32 — Whether lenders to group entities (RCom and RTL) could be recognised as Financial Creditors of Reliance Infratel Ltd. (RITL) based on Deeds of Hypothecation (DoH) executed under a Master Security Trustee Agreement (MSTA) — Held, DoH (clause 5(iii)) embodies a promise by Chargors, including RITL, to pay shortfall/deficiency after enforcement and realisation, amounting to a guarantee within Section 126 ; hence, liability in respect of such guarantee is a financial debt under Section 5(8)(i) — Default is not a precondition to submit a claim; moratorium under Section 14 does not extinguish claims — NCLAT’s contrary view set aside; NCLT’s order dated 02.03.2021 restored ; appeals allowed ; alternative plea on classification as secured creditors not examined.

### Case Summary
Summary Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 — Sections 3(6), 3(11), 3(12), 5(7), 5(8)(i), 7(1), 14, 15(1) ; Indian Contract Act, 1872 — Sections 126, 127, 32 — Whether lenders to group entities (RCom and RTL) could be recognised as Financial Creditors of Reliance Infratel Ltd. (RITL) based on Deeds of Hypothecation (DoH) executed under a Master Security Trustee Agreement (MSTA) — Held, DoH (clause 5(iii)) embodies a promise by Chargors, including RITL, to pay shortfall/deficiency after enforcement and realisation, amounting to a guarantee within Section 126 ; hence, liability in respect of such guarantee is a financial debt under Section 5(8)(i) — Default is not a precondition to submit a claim; moratorium under Section 14 does not extinguish claims — NCLAT’s contrary view set aside; NCLT’s order dated 02.03.2021 restored ; appeals allowed ; alternative plea on classification as secured creditors not examined.
