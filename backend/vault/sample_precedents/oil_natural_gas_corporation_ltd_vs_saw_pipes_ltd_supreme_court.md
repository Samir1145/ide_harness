# Oil Natural Gas Corporation Ltd Vs. Saw Pipes Ltd
* **Citation:** (2017) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2003-04-16
* **Parties:** SC and Associate Builders v. Delhi Development Authority

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 – Section 34 – Scope of Court’s jurisdiction to set aside arbitral awards – Held that the Court may set aside an arbitral award under Section 34 if it is patently illegal, in contravention of the provisions of the Act, any other substantive law governing the parties, or against the terms of the contract, and that the phrase “public policy of India” under Section 34(2)(b)(ii) must be given a wider meaning to include awards that are contrary to fundamental policy of Indian law, the interest of India, justice or morality, or are patently illegal. In the present case, the arbitral award directing ONGC to refund liquidated damages was set aside as it was contrary to the express terms of the contract and the statutory provisions, particularly Sections 73 and 74 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, and Section 28(3) of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Appeal allowed; impugned award set aside.
