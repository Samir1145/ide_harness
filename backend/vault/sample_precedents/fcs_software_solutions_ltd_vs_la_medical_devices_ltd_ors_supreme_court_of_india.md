# Fcs Software Solutions Ltd Vs. La Medical Devices Ltd Ors
* **Citation:** (2017) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2008-07-08
* **Parties:** Sons v. Sri Ramanya Das &amp

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Whether a Company Court can direct a fresh auction and set aside an earlier accepted or even confirmed sale of a company in liquidation where the original sale process suffered from defects such as absence of valuation, reserve price, and inventory disclosure, and a substantially higher bid was later received? Company Court Rules, 1959 – Rule 9 ; Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 – Section 151 – Company in liquidation – Court sale of company property – Fresh auction after acceptance/confirmation of earlier bid – Validity of resale on ground of defects in first auction process and receipt of substantially higher offer – Appellant was highest bidder at Rs.1.47 crores in first sale process and deposited full consideration – before possession was handed over, objections were raised that sale notice did not disclose valuation of movable and immovable assets, reserve price was not fixed, and inventory of plant and machinery was not made available – Company Judge ordered fresh auction – in resale respondent offered Rs.3.5 crores and its bid was accepted – HELD : condition of court confirmation is a safeguard against inadequate price and the Company Court must ensure that property of a company in liquidation fetches the highest reasonable price for satisfaction of claims of creditors and others concerned – in view of defects in the first auction and non-disclosure of material particulars, direction for fresh auction was proper – appellant having declined opportunity to raise its bid could not insist on purchase at Rs.1.47 crores – sale in favour of respondent No. 3 upheld – appeal partly allowed only by directing payment of Rs.30 lakhs to appellant as solatium.
