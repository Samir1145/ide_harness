# National Spot Exchange Ltd Vs. Union Of India And Ors
* **Citation:** (2025) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2025-05-14
* **Parties:** In Supreme Court Bar Association Vs. Union of India &amp

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Constitution of India – Article 142 – SARFAESI Act, 2002 – RDB Act, 1993 – MPID Act, 1999 – PMLA, 2002 – IBC, 2016 – Priority of secured creditors vis-à-vis assets attached under MPID Act and PMLA – Whether secured creditors have priority over assets attached under MPID Act and PMLA by virtue of SARFAESI Act and RDB Act; and whether properties attached under MPID Act are available for execution of decrees despite moratorium under IBC – Held: The MPID Act is a valid State legislation and prevails over the SARFAESI Act and RDB Act in its field; secured creditors cannot claim priority over properties attached under the MPID Act ; there is no inconsistency between the IBC and MPID Act ; properties attached under the MPID Act are available for execution of decrees by the Supreme Court Committee despite moratorium under Section 14 of IBC ; both impugned orders of the Supreme Court Committee are upheld .
