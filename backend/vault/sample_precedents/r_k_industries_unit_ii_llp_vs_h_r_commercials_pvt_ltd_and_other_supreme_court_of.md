# R K Industries Unit Ii Llp Vs. H R Commercials Pvt Ltd And Other
* **Citation:** (2022) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2022-08-25
* **Parties:** LLP v. H

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 —Sections 33, 34, 35 and Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016 —Regulations 8, 31A, 32, 33—Sale of assets by Liquidator—Scope of judicial review—The Supreme Court held that the Liquidator is empowered to sell the assets of the Corporate Debtor in liquidation by public auction or private contract, collectively or in piecemeal, with the objective of value maximization and timely realization for stakeholders. The Liquidator’s commercial decisions, including discontinuing the Second Swiss Challenge Process and opting for Private Sale, are not amenable to judicial review unless shown to be arbitrary or mala fide. The NCLAT’s direction to restart the Private Sale process with open notice to all prospective buyers was set aside, and the Private Sale process as approved by the NCLT was directed to be concluded expeditiously. Civil Appeal No. 7722 of 2021 dismissed; Civil Appeal No. 7731 of 2021 allowed.
