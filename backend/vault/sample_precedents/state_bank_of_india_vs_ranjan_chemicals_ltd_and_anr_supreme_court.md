# State Bank Of India Vs. Ranjan Chemicals Ltd And Anr
* **Citation:** (2017) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2006-10-10
* **Parties:** relied on United Bank of India v. Abhijit Tea Co

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Recovery of Debts Due to Banks and Financial Institutions Act, 1993 – Section 19(6)–(11) – jurisdiction and power of Debt Recovery Tribunal to entertain set-off and counter-claim ; scope of treating an independent suit as counter-claim/cross-suit; power and basis for ordering joint trial and transfer of civil suit – Bank filed O.A. before DRT for recovery of dues under term loan and cash credit facility; borrower-company filed earlier civil suit claiming large damages alleging bank’s failure to fulfil obligations in relation to cash credit and rehabilitation package – Trial Court and High Court refused transfer, proceeding on civil court jurisdiction and treating company’s suit as independent and unconnected – Supreme Court held both claims arose from same transaction/cause of action and company’s damages claim was in essence a set-off/counter-claim within Section 19 ; relied on United Bank of India v. Abhijit Tea Co. Pvt. Ltd. and distinguished approach in Indian Bank v. ABS Marine Products (P) Ltd. regarding joint trial and need for consent – affirmed inherent power of Court to order joint trial based on convenience, avoidance of multiplicity and overlapping evidence, without parties’ consent – held DRT competent to try both bank’s claim and company’s counter-claim, and civil suit could be transferred but DRT proceedings could not be transferred to civil court – appeal allowed , orders of Trial Court and High Court set aside, and civil Money Suit transferred to DRT, Patna to be treated as counter-claim/cross-suit and tried jointly with bank’s O.A., with parties to bear their own costs.
