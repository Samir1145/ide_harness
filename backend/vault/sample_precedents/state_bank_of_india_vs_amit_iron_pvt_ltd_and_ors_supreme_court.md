# State Bank Of India Vs. Amit Iron Pvt Ltd And Ors
* **Citation:** (2026) SC
* **Court:** IN THE SUPREME COURT
* **Date:** 2026-04-06
* **Parties:** Held that the principles of natural justice, as read into the RBI Master Directions by the Supreme Court in State Bank of India v. Rajesh Agarwal, require issuance of a show cause notice, supply of the relied upon material

### Headnote / Legal Dictum
HeadNote Banking Regulation Act, 1949 – Section 35A – Reserve Bank of India (Frauds Classification and Reporting by Commercial Banks and Select FIs) Directions, 2016 and Reserve Bank of India (Fraud Risk Management in Commercial Banks (including Regional Rural Banks) and All India Financial Institutions Directions, 2024 – Whether the principles of natural justice require a personal/oral hearing to be afforded to borrowers before their accounts are classified as “fraud” by banks, and whether borrowers are entitled to the entire forensic audit report relied upon for such classification – Held that the principles of natural justice, as read into the RBI Master Directions by the Supreme Court in State Bank of India v. Rajesh Agarwal, require issuance of a show cause notice, supply of the relied upon material (including the forensic audit report), an opportunity to submit a written representation, and a reasoned order, but do not mandate a personal/oral hearing as of right. The supply of the forensic audit report is mandatory, subject to limited exceptions for third-party confidentiality. The RBI’s 2024 Master Directions correctly incorporate these requirements. Appeals partly allowed : directions for personal hearing set aside; directions for supply of forensic audit reports upheld.
