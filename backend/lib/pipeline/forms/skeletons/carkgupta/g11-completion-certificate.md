# G11: Completion Certificate
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 117`

G11   Completion Certificate

Section 117

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

That the resolution professional shall issue a certificate on the completion of the repayment plan.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the certificate, nor for the accompanying statement of what was actually paid against what was promised.



WHY IT MATTERS

Discharge follows from this certificate. A certificate unsupported by a payment reconciliation cannot support a discharge that will hold.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional under Section 117. Draft a completion certificate for a repayment plan, accompanied by a reconciliation of amounts promised against amounts actually paid, creditor by creditor, with the dates of payment. Record any shortfall and how it was dealt with. Identify the creditors to whom the certificate must be served.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
