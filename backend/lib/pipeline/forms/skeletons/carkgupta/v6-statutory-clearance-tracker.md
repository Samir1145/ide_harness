# V6: Statutory Clearance Tracker
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 59 | Regulations 37, 38`

V6   Statutory Clearance Tracker

Section 59 | Regulations 37, 38

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

Nothing. The requirement to obtain clearances arises from the other statutes, not from the Code.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the tracker recording each clearance applied for, its status, and the effect of a pending clearance on the dissolution timeline.



WHY IT MATTERS

Voluntary liquidations overrun almost entirely because of pending statutory clearances. What is not tracked is not chased.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a voluntary liquidation liquidator. Draft a statutory clearance tracker covering income tax, goods and services tax, provident fund, employee state insurance, the Registrar of Companies and any sector regulator. For each show the application date, the authority, the current status, the officer contacted, the next follow-up date and the effect of the pendency on dissolution.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Authority and the clearance sought

Date of application and the acknowledgement reference

Current status and the last communication

Officer or office being followed up with

Next follow-up date

Pending assessments or proceedings, if any

Effect of the pendency on the dissolution timeline

Provision made in the accounts, if any

Escalation taken, where the delay is unexplained
