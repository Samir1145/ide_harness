# G8: Notice of the Meeting of Creditors
**Statutory Authority:** `Sections 111, 112, 113`

G8   Notice of the Meeting of Creditors

Sections 111, 112, 113

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The period of notice, and the documents that must accompany it.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the notice or for the accompanying statement of the debtor's affairs.



WHY IT MATTERS

Creditors vote on the plan on the strength of what accompanies the notice. A thin notice produces a rejection that better disclosure would have avoided.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional under Section 111. Draft a notice of the meeting of creditors together with the accompanying statement of the debtor's affairs. Cover the date, time and place, the plan as proposed, the report under Section 106, the voting mechanism and thresholds, and the consequence of the plan being rejected. Cite the provision for the notice period.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Date, time, place and mode of the meeting

Notice period computed against the statute

The repayment plan as proposed

The Section 106 report, enclosed

Statement of the debtor's affairs

List of creditors and voting shares

Voting mechanism and the majority required

Consequence if the plan is not approved

Proof of service on each creditor
