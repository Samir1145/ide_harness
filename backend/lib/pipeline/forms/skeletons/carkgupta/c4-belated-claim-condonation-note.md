# C4: Belated Claim — Condonation Note
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 12(2)`

C4   Belated Claim — Condonation Note

Regulation 12(2)

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The outer window within which a belated claim may be considered.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the reasoned note by which the resolution professional decides whether to condone the delay and admit the claim.



WHY IT MATTERS

The discretion must be seen to have been exercised. Where no note exists, the resolution professional has nothing to place before the Bench except an assertion.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional. Draft a reasoned note on a claim filed after the last date announced in Form A. Record the date of the announcement, the date of receipt, the length and explanation of the delay, the stage the process has reached, the effect on the information memorandum and the voting shares, and the decision with reasons. Cite Regulation 12.
