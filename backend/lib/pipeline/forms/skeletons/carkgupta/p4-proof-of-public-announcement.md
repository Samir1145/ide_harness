# P4: Proof of Public Announcement
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 54E | PPIRP Regulations`

P4   Proof of Public Announcement

Section 54E | PPIRP Regulations

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The form of the public announcement and the period within which it is to be made.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the record proving publication, which matters more here because the timeline is compressed.



WHY IT MATTERS

The pre-packaged timeline runs from commencement in days, not months. Any dispute about when the announcement was made consumes time the process does not have.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional in a pre-packaged process. Prepare a proof of publication record for the public announcement under Section 54E, capturing each medium with the date and time of publication, the website uploads, the intimation to the Board, and the resulting last date for claims computed against the statutory period.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
