# P11: Termination Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Sections 54N, 54O`

P11   Termination Record

Sections 54N, 54O

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The grounds on which the process may be terminated and the consequences that follow.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the record supporting the termination, nor for the statement of what happens to the steps already taken.



WHY IT MATTERS

Termination is not the end of the matter. It may be followed by liquidation, and the record made at termination is what the next professional inherits.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional in a pre-packaged process. Draft the record supporting a termination under Section 54N, covering the ground relied on, the committee's vote, the position of claims already admitted, the costs incurred and their treatment, what happens to the management of the corporate debtor, and what is to be handed over.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
