# V11: Termination Reasoning Note
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 59(5A)`

V11   Termination Reasoning Note

Section 59(5A)

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

Form J, introduced by the 2026 amendments, for the termination of the voluntary liquidation process.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the reasoning note that must support the termination, nor for the intimation to stakeholders and the record of the reversal of steps already taken.



WHY IT MATTERS

The form records the fact of termination. It does not record why, nor what happens to the steps already taken. That reasoning is what will be examined afterwards.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a voluntary liquidation liquidator. Draft the reasoning note supporting a termination of the voluntary liquidation process under Section 59(5A), covering the ground for termination, the resolution of the members, the position of creditors, the treatment of claims already admitted, the reversal of steps already taken, and the intimations to be issued. Identify what Form J does not capture.
