# G10: Modification Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 115`

G10   Modification Record

Section 115

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

That the plan may be modified at the meeting with the debtor's consent.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for recording the modification itself, the debtor's consent to it, and the plan as it stands after modification.



WHY IT MATTERS

The Adjudicating Authority approves the plan as modified. If the modification is recorded loosely, the approved plan is uncertain from the day it is approved.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional under Section 115. Draft a modification record showing the clause as originally proposed, the modification, who proposed it, the debtor's consent with its date, and the clause as it stands after modification. Produce the consolidated plan as modified, so that what is placed before the Bench is a single complete document.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
