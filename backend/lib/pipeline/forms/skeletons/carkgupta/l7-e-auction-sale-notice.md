# L7: e-Auction Sale Notice
**Statutory Authority:** `Schedule I, Liquidation Process Regulations`

L7   e-Auction Sale Notice

Schedule I, Liquidation Process Regulations

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The mode of sale, the auction process and the notice period.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the sale notice itself, nor for the terms and conditions document that accompanies it.



WHY IT MATTERS

The notice defines what the buyer takes and on what footing. Ambiguity here surfaces only at the registrar's office, months later.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft an e-auction sale notice and terms and conditions under Schedule I. Cover the asset description and identification, the reserve price and EMD, inspection dates, the as-is-where-is and as-is-what-is basis, known encumbrances, the incremental bid value, the payment schedule with the consequence of default, and the point at which the sale certificate issues.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Asset description with survey or identification numbers

Reserve price, EMD and the incremental bid value

Inspection dates and the contact for inspection

As-is-where-is, as-is-what-is, whatever-there-is basis

Known encumbrances and statutory dues disclosed

Auction platform, date and the auto-extension rule

Payment schedule and consequences of default

Point at which the sale certificate will issue

Liquidator's right to cancel, and the disclaimer
