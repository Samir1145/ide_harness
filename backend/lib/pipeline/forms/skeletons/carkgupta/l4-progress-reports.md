# L4: Progress Reports
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 15`

L4   Progress Reports

Regulation 15

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The contents and the periodicity of the reports.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format, although the reports run through the entire process and are read cumulatively.



WHY IT MATTERS

Inconsistency between successive progress reports is the most common inspection finding against liquidators.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft a quarterly progress report under Regulation 15 in a form that carries forward the closing position of the previous report as its opening position. Cover realisation, distribution, litigation, the consultation committee, liquidation cost against estimate, and the reasons for any change in the projected timeline.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Period, and the opening position carried forward

Assets realised in the period, and cumulatively

Amounts distributed, and to which class

Liquidation cost — estimated, incurred, paid

Consultation committee meetings held and advice received

Litigation — filed, pending, disposed

Claims position and any revision

Change in the projected timeline, with reasons

Statement of the liquidator's own remuneration drawn
