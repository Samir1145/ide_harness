# L10: Unclaimed Proceeds Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 53 | Regulation 46`

L10   Unclaimed Proceeds Record

Section 53 | Regulation 46

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The obligation to deposit unclaimed and undistributed proceeds into the Corporate Liquidation Account, with a statement in the prescribed manner.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the liquidator's own record correlating each unclaimed amount to the stakeholder, the attempts made to pay, and the reason the amount remains unclaimed.



WHY IT MATTERS

Claims against the account arise years after dissolution, when the liquidator has moved on. Without the correlation record, nobody can answer them.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft a record of unclaimed and undistributed proceeds for deposit into the Corporate Liquidation Account. For each amount show the stakeholder, the class, the amount, the date it fell due, every attempt made to pay, the reason it remains unclaimed, and the last known particulars. Structure it so that a claimant approaching the Board years later can be identified from it.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
