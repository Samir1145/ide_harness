# C13: IRP to RP Handover Memorandum
**Statutory Authority:** `Sections 22, 23 | IP Code of Conduct`

C13   IRP to RP Handover Memorandum

Sections 22, 23 | IP Code of Conduct

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The appointment mechanism, and the obligation to co-operate.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the handover of records, custody, bank operations, pending applications and undertakings on a change of professional.



WHY IT MATTERS

The incoming professional inherits liability for everything that is not documented at the point of handover.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to an incoming resolution professional. Draft a handover memorandum recording the transfer from the outgoing professional. Cover statutory records, claims file, CoC records, bank accounts and signatories, insurance, employees, pending applications with next dates, unpaid CIRP costs, and a schedule of documents not handed over. Include an acknowledgement block for both professionals.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Date, place and mode of handover

Statutory records and books received — schedule-wise

Claims file, verification memos and list of creditors

CoC records, minutes and voting records

Bank accounts, balances, signatories and mandates

Insurance policies and their status

Employees, payroll and statutory dues position

Pending applications with next dates of hearing

CIRP costs incurred, paid and outstanding

Schedule of documents NOT handed over

Acknowledgement by both professionals
