# C6: CoC Meeting Notice and Minutes
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulations 19, 21, 24(7)`

C6   CoC Meeting Notice and Minutes

Regulations 19, 21, 24(7)

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The contents of the notice in Regulation 21 and the obligation to circulate minutes within the stipulated period.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for either the notice or the minutes.



WHY IT MATTERS

The minutes are the primary evidence before the Adjudicating Authority of what the committee decided and on what material. Loose minutes are the single most expensive habit in Indian insolvency practice.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional. Draft the minutes of a meeting of the committee of creditors. Record attendance with voting shares, quorum, the agenda item-wise discussion, the material placed before the committee, the resolution as put to vote, the result member-wise with voting shares, and the action points with owners and dates. Do not summarise the discussion into a single sentence.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Meeting number, date, time, mode and venue

Attendance — members, voting shares, ARs, invitees

Quorum recorded against Regulation 22

Agenda item-wise: material placed, discussion, resolution

Voting — e-voting window, member-wise result, voting share

Decisions carried, not carried, deferred

Action points with owner and date

Confirmation of the previous meeting's minutes

Circulation record within the prescribed period
