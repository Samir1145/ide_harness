# P5: Management Transfer Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 54J`

P5   Management Transfer Record

Section 54J

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The circumstances in which management of the corporate debtor vests in the resolution professional.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the record of the transfer itself — custody, bank operations, statutory records and the point at which control actually passed.



WHY IT MATTERS

Section 54J transfers management on an order. The moment control passes is a question of fact, and it is answered by the record made on the day.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional in a pre-packaged process. Draft a management transfer record under Section 54J, capturing the order and its date, the handover of custody, bank accounts and signatories, statutory records, employees, and an express statement of the time at which control passed. Include acknowledgements from the outgoing management.
