# G7: Report on the Repayment Plan
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 106`

G7   Report on the Repayment Plan

Section 106

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

That the resolution professional shall submit a report on whether the plan should be considered, and whether a meeting of creditors is required.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the report — including where the plan offers a very small amount and the professional must nonetheless take a position.



WHY IT MATTERS

The professional cannot simply forward a poor plan. Where the amount offered is meagre, the report must reason to a recommendation, and there is no prescribed structure for that reasoning.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional under Section 106. Draft a report on a repayment plan that offers a small amount relative to the admitted debt. Set out the amount admitted, the amount offered and the percentage, the debtor's disclosed assets and income, the comparison with bankruptcy, whether the plan meets Section 105, whether a meeting of creditors is required, and a reasoned recommendation. Do not soften the arithmetic.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Admitted debt, creditor-wise

Amount offered under the plan and the percentage

Debtor's disclosed assets, income and dependants

Verification of the disclosures made

Comparison with the likely outcome on bankruptcy

Whether the plan provides for the Section 105 matters

Whether a meeting of creditors is required, and why

Reasoned recommendation, stated plainly

Date, and the statutory period within which submitted
