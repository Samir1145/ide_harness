V4   Preliminary Report

Regulation 9, Voluntary Liquidation Regulations

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The contents and the period within which the report is to be submitted.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format.



WHY IT MATTERS

It is the liquidator's own confirmation that the solvency position declared by the directors actually holds.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a voluntary liquidation liquidator. Draft a preliminary report under Regulation 9 covering the capital structure, the estimated assets and liabilities, whether further inquiry is required, the proposed plan of action with a timeline, and the estimated liquidation cost. Add an express statement on whether the solvency declared by the directors is borne out.
