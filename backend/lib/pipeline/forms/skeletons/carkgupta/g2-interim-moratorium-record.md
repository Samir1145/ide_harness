# G2: Interim Moratorium Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 96`

G2   Interim Moratorium Record

Section 96

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

That an interim moratorium commences on the filing of the application, and what it covers.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the record identifying the proceedings and actions actually stayed, and the intimations issued.



WHY IT MATTERS

The interim moratorium operates automatically, but nobody is bound by what they do not know. The record is what converts an automatic stay into an effective one.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional under Part III. Draft a record of the interim moratorium under Section 96, identifying the date of filing, every pending proceeding in respect of the debt, which of them are stayed and which are not, the intimations issued to each forum and creditor, and any action taken in breach.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
