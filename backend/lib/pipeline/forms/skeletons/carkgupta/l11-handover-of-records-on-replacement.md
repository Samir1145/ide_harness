# L11: Handover of Records on Replacement
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 45A(4)`

L11   Handover of Records on Replacement

Regulation 45A(4)

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The obligation on the outgoing liquidator to hand over records to the successor.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the handover schedule, and no format for the successor's record of what was not received.



WHY IT MATTERS

This is now a live litigating point. A successor liquidator who has not documented what was withheld cannot make out a case under Section 60(5).

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a successor liquidator. Draft a handover schedule under Regulation 45A(4) together with a schedule of records demanded but not received, dated and correlated to each written demand. Structure it so that it can be annexed to an application under Section 60(5). Keep the two schedules strictly separate.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Order appointing the successor liquidator

Written demands made — date and mode of each

Schedule A — records actually received

Schedule B — records demanded but not received

Bank accounts, balances and signatory change

Statutory records, minutes and the claims file

Pending litigation and case files

Liquidation estate account and its reconciliation

Effect of the non-handover on the process

Correlation of Schedule B to each written demand
