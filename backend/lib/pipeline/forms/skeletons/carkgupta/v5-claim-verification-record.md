# V5: Claim Verification Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulations 17 to 22, Voluntary Liquidation Regulations`

V5   Claim Verification Record

Regulations 17 to 22, Voluntary Liquidation Regulations

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The forms in which claims are to be submitted by each class of stakeholder.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the liquidator's verification record, nor for the list of stakeholders that follows from it.



WHY IT MATTERS

The whole premise of a voluntary liquidation is that every claim will be paid in full. A claim wrongly verified defeats the solvency on which the process rests.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a voluntary liquidation liquidator. Draft a claim verification record and the consequent list of stakeholders. For each claim record the form, the amount claimed, the documents relied on, the cross-check against the company's books, the amount admitted and any amount rejected with reasons. Add a reconciliation confirming that admitted claims remain within the realisable assets.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Claim serial number, form type and date of receipt

Stakeholder class and identity, masked in the AI draft

Amount claimed — principal, interest, other

Documents relied on and further documents called for

Cross-check against the company's books

Amount admitted and amount rejected with reasons

List of stakeholders, class-wise

Reconciliation of admitted claims to realisable assets

Confirmation that solvency continues to hold
