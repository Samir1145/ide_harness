# L12: Final Report
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 45`

L12   Final Report

Regulation 45

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The contents of the final report and the obligation to submit it with the dissolution application.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format.



WHY IT MATTERS

It is the document on which the Bench dissolves the corporate debtor, and the document against which the liquidator's conduct is judged afterwards.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft a final report under Regulation 45. Cover the audited accounts of the liquidation, the manner in which assets were sold with the material particulars of each sale, the shortfall against the amount due to stakeholders, and a statement that all assets have been liquidated and no litigation is pending or, where it is, the effect of it.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Audited receipts and payments of the liquidation

Sale-wise particulars — asset, mode, buyer, realisation

Comparison of realisation against valuation

Distribution made, class-wise, under Section 53

Shortfall against the amount due to each class

Liquidation cost and the liquidator's remuneration

Litigation pending, if any, and its effect

Statement that the estate is fully liquidated

Unclaimed dividends and the Corporate Liquidation Account

Prayer for dissolution

YOUR HOUSE VARIATIONS









Voluntary Liquidation

Section 59 of the Code, read with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (Voluntary Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2017 — including the termination route introduced by the 2026 amendments.

WHAT IS PRESCRIBED IN A VOLUNTARY LIQUIDATION

Requirement

Prescribed form

Public announcement

Form A under Schedule I

Submission of claims

The forms in the Schedule, by class

Termination of the process

Form J, introduced by the 2026 amendments

Filings with the Board

The VL monitoring forms notified in 2026

Application for dissolution

Form NCLT-1, being a generic form



The declaration of solvency, on which the entire process rests, has no prescribed format.
