# V7: Distribution and Payment Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 34, Voluntary Liquidation Regulations`

V7   Distribution and Payment Record

Regulation 34, Voluntary Liquidation Regulations

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The period within which the proceeds are to be distributed to stakeholders.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the record showing what was paid to each stakeholder, when, and against which admitted claim.



WHY IT MATTERS

The final report asserts that no creditor stands prejudiced. That assertion is only as good as the payment record supporting it.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a voluntary liquidation liquidator. Draft a distribution and payment record showing, for each stakeholder, the admitted claim, the amount paid, the date and mode of payment, the instrument reference and the acknowledgement obtained. Add a reconciliation to the liquidation bank account and a statement of amounts remaining unpaid with the reason.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.

Stakeholder-wise admitted claim and amount paid

Acknowledgement or confirmation obtained

Payments to members after creditors are paid in full

Compliance with the period in Regulation 34
