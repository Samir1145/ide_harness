# G6: Repayment Plan
**Statutory Authority:** `Sections 105, 106`

G6   Repayment Plan

Sections 105, 106

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The matters the plan must provide for, and the requirement of the resolution professional's report on it.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the repayment plan itself.



WHY IT MATTERS

This is the central document of Part III and it has no prescribed shape at all. In practice, plans arrive as a letter.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a debtor preparing a repayment plan under Section 105. Draft the structure covering the justification for the plan, the sources of funds, the term, the treatment of each class of creditor, the proposed distribution and its timing, the role of the resolution professional in supervising implementation, and the consequence of default. Address every matter Section 105 requires the plan to provide for.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
