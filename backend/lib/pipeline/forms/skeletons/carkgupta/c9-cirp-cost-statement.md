# C9: CIRP Cost Statement
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulations 31, 33, 34, 34A`

C9   CIRP Cost Statement

Regulations 31, 33, 34, 34A

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

What constitutes insolvency resolution process cost, and that fee is to be fixed by the committee.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the periodic cost statement placed before the committee for ratification.



WHY IT MATTERS

Costs not approved head-wise and in advance are routinely disallowed, and the professional bears them personally.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional. Draft a CIRP cost statement for approval by the committee of creditors. Present head-wise: RP fee, support staff, valuers, legal, publication, insurance, IU fees, and costs of running the corporate debtor as a going concern. For each head show approved, incurred, paid and outstanding, with the meeting number at which approval was taken.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
