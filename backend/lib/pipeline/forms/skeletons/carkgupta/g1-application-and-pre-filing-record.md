# G1: Application and Pre-Filing Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Sections 94, 95 | Insolvency and Bankruptcy (Application to Adjudicating Authority for Insolvency Resolution Process for Personal Guarantors) Rules, 2019`

G1   Application and Pre-Filing Record

Sections 94, 95 | Insolvency and Bankruptcy (Application to Adjudicating Authority for Insolvency Resolution Process for Personal Guarantors) Rules, 2019

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The forms in which the application is to be made by the debtor or by the creditor, and the documents to accompany it.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the pre-filing record establishing the guarantee, its invocation, the demand and the default, correlated to the annexures.



WHY IT MATTERS

Applications against personal guarantors fail at the threshold on the guarantee and its invocation far more often than on the merits.

PROMPT

Act as counsel filing an application against a personal guarantor. Draft a pre-filing record establishing the principal debt, the deed of guarantee with its operative clauses, the invocation, the demand and its service, the default and its date, and limitation. Correlate each to the annexure that evidences it and flag every gap in the chain.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Principal debt and the underlying facility

Deed of guarantee — date, parties, operative clauses

Whether the guarantee is continuing and co-extensive

Invocation — date, mode and the notice relied on

Demand, its service and proof of service

Default, its date and the amount in default

Limitation and any acknowledgement relied on

Annexure correlation for each element

Gaps in the chain, flagged
