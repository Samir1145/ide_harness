# L6: Secured Creditor Relinquishment Intimation
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 52 | Regulation 21A`

L6   Secured Creditor Relinquishment Intimation

Section 52 | Regulation 21A

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The consequence of failing to intimate the decision within the stipulated period.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the intimation itself, nor for the liquidator's record of the consequences that follow non-relinquishment.



WHY IT MATTERS

The election is irreversible and drives the entire waterfall. It is made by a letter that has no prescribed form.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft the intimation to be issued to secured creditors calling for their election under Section 52 read with Regulation 21A, and a companion record capturing each creditor's election, the date, the effect on the liquidation estate, and the amounts payable by a creditor realising its security outside the process. Cite the sub-regulation for each consequence.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
