# C8: Valuer Engagement and Valuation Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulations 27, 35`

C8   Valuer Engagement and Valuation Record

Regulations 27, 35

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The qualification of registered valuers and the requirement to determine fair value and liquidation value.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the engagement letter, the scope of work, or the resolution professional's record of receipt and custody of the two values.



WHY IT MATTERS

Premature disclosure of fair value or liquidation value is a disciplinary matter. The custody record is the professional's protection.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional. Draft a letter of engagement for two registered valuers under Regulation 27, covering scope, asset classes, standard of value, site access, timelines, fee, confidentiality and independence declarations. Separately draft a custody record for the receipt of fair value and liquidation value under Regulation 35, recording sealed receipt, custody and the point of permitted disclosure.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Engagement letter — scope and asset classes

Standard of value and the valuation date

Independence and eligibility declaration by the valuer

Confidentiality undertaking and non-disclosure

Timeline and fee, and whether it is a CIRP cost

Custody record — date of receipt of each report, sealed

Whether the two estimates were significantly different

Record of any third valuation commissioned

Point at which disclosure to the CoC was permitted
