# V10: Final Report
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 38`

V10   Final Report

Regulation 38

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The contents of the final report to be sent to the contributories, the Board and the Adjudicating Authority.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format.



WHY IT MATTERS

It is filed with the dissolution application and is the document on which the company ceases to exist.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a voluntary liquidation liquidator. Draft a final report under Regulation 38, covering the audited accounts of the liquidation, the manner of disposal of assets, a statement that the liquidation has been completed without prejudice to any creditor, and the details of the sale of assets with the persons to whom they were sold.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Audited receipts and payments of the liquidation

Manner in which the assets were disposed of

Persons to whom the assets were sold

Claims received, admitted and paid in full

Statement that no creditor stands prejudiced

Statutory clearances obtained

Liquidation cost and the liquidator's remuneration

Unclaimed proceeds and the Corporate Voluntary Liquidation Account

Prayer for dissolution
