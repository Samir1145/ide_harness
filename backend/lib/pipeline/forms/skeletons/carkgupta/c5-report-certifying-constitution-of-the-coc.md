# C5: Report Certifying Constitution of the CoC
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 17(1)`

C5   Report Certifying Constitution of the CoC

Regulation 17(1)

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The obligation to file the report with the Adjudicating Authority within the stipulated period.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the report itself.



WHY IT MATTERS

This single report fixes the voting shares on which every subsequent decision rests. An error here unravels the CoC's commercial wisdom retrospectively.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to an interim resolution professional. Draft a report certifying the constitution of the committee of creditors under Regulation 17(1). Cover claims received and verified, related party exclusions under the proviso to Section 21(2), classes of creditors and authorised representatives, the voting share computation, and a certificate that the CoC has been constituted in accordance with Section 21.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
