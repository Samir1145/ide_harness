# V1: Resolution Sequence Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 59(3)`

V1   Resolution Sequence Record

Section 59(3)

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The declaration, the special resolution and, where the company owes debt, the approval of creditors representing two-thirds in value.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the composite record showing that each step was taken, in the correct order, and within the periods the section allows.



WHY IT MATTERS

Section 59 is a sequence. A voluntary liquidation begun with the steps out of order is defective from commencement, and the defect surfaces only when dissolution is sought.

PROMPT

Act as counsel advising a company proposing voluntary liquidation. Draft a sequence compliance record under Section 59(3), listing each pre-condition, the date on which it was satisfied and the document evidencing it, arranged in the statutory order. Compute the interval between each step against the period the section allows. Flag any step taken out of sequence or outside the period.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
