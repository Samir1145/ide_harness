# C10: Information Memorandum
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 29 | Regulation 36`

C10   Information Memorandum

Section 29 | Regulation 36

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The list of contents in Regulation 36(2), and the confidentiality undertaking before it is shared.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format. Regulation 36(2) is a checklist of contents, not a structure for the document.



WHY IT MATTERS

The information memorandum is the longest single document produced in a CIRP, and the quality of the resolution plans received is a direct function of its quality.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional. Draft the structure of an information memorandum under Regulation 36, mapping every item in Regulation 36(2) to a numbered chapter. Add an assumptions and limitations note, a data room index, and a statement of what has not been verified. Produce a table showing each sub-clause of Regulation 36(2) against the chapter that answers it.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
