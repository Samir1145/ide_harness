# G4: Resolution Professional's Report under Section 99
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 99, Part III`

G4   Resolution Professional's Report under Section 99

Section 99, Part III

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

That the resolution professional shall examine the application and submit a report recommending acceptance or rejection.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the report.



WHY IT MATTERS

The Adjudicating Authority admits or rejects the application on this report. It carries the entire weight of the examination.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional appointed under Section 97. Draft a report under Section 99 examining an application by a personal guarantor. Cover the existence of the debt and the default, the guarantee invoked, the eligibility of the applicant, the information sought and furnished, and a reasoned recommendation to accept or reject, with the reasons stated separately from the facts.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Appointment and the date of reference

Particulars of the debt and the default

The guarantee, its invocation and the demand

Information sought from the applicant and the response

Whether the debt is excluded or unenforceable

Eligibility of the applicant

Facts found, stated separately from the reasoning

Reasoned recommendation — acceptance or rejection

Date of submission within the statutory period
