# L8: Sale Certificate
**Statutory Authority:** `Schedule I | Section 17(2)(xii), Registration Act 1908`

L8   Sale Certificate

Schedule I | Section 17(2)(xii), Registration Act 1908

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

That the asset shall be sold, and that the sale certificate issues on receipt of the full consideration.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the sale certificate itself — the instrument by which title actually moves.



WHY IT MATTERS

The registration and stamp position on a liquidation sale certificate remains contested in practice. The drafting of the certificate is what the sub-registrar reads, and it is unprescribed.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft a sale certificate for an immovable asset sold in liquidation. Recite the liquidation order, the authority to sell, the auction process and dates, the successful bidder and the consideration received in full, and the schedule of the property. Address the basis on which the certificate is presented for registration, and set out the statutory provisions relied on. State the position neutrally where it is contested.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Recital — liquidation order, date and Bench

Authority of the liquidator to sell

Auction process, notice dates and the platform

Successful bidder and the bid amount

Confirmation that consideration has been received in full

Schedule of the property with boundaries

Basis of transfer — as-is-where-is, encumbrances disclosed

Provision relied on for registration and stamp treatment

Delivery of possession and the date

Execution, attestation and the liquidator's seal
