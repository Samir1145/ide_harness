# C1: Proof of Public Announcement
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 15 | Regulation 6, CIRP Regulations`

C1   Proof of Public Announcement

Section 15 | Regulation 6, CIRP Regulations

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

Form A — the text of the public announcement itself.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the IRP's record proving that Form A was in fact published in two newspapers, on the corporate debtor's website and on the IBBI website.



WHY IT MATTERS

This is among the first items called for in an inspection. The announcement is prescribed; the evidence that it was published is not.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to an interim resolution professional. Prepare a Proof of Publication record for a Form A public announcement under Regulation 6. Capture newspaper names, editions, languages, dates and page numbers, website URLs with upload dates, and the IBBI portal intimation. Output as a signed record with an annexure index. Cite the sub-regulations relied on.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.

Newspaper 1 — name, edition, language, date, page

Last date for submission of claims as announced
