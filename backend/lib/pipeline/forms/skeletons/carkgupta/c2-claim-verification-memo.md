# C2: Claim Verification Memo
**Statutory Authority:** `Sections 18(1)(b), 21(1) | Regulations 10, 12, 13`

C2   Claim Verification Memo

Sections 18(1)(b), 21(1) | Regulations 10, 12, 13

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

Forms B, C, CA, D, E and F for the submission of claims by each class of creditor.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the IRP's or RP's own verification note recording how each claim was tested, and why it was admitted, partly admitted or rejected.



WHY IT MATTERS

Regulation 13 requires verification within seven days. Rejection without a reasoned contemporaneous record is the commonest ground on which a resolution professional is dragged before the Adjudicating Authority.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional. Draft a claim verification memo for a claim received in Form C. Record the documents relied on, the cross-check against the corporate debtor's books, the computation of principal and interest, the amount admitted, the amount rejected with reasons, and the date of verification. Cite Regulations 10 and 13. Flag every item you cannot verify from the material supplied.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
