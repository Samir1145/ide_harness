# C14: Resolution Plan Structure
**Statutory Authority:** `Sections 30, 31 | Regulations 37, 38, 39`

C14   Resolution Plan Structure

Sections 30, 31 | Regulations 37, 38, 39

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The mandatory contents in Regulation 38, and Form H, the compliance certificate.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the resolution plan itself.



WHY IT MATTERS

Non-conforming plans consume weeks of committee time and invite objections at approval. A house structure circulated with the RFRP saves the process.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional. Draft a resolution plan structure that maps every requirement of Regulations 37, 38 and 39 and Section 30(2) to a numbered clause. Add a compliance table showing each statutory requirement against the clause that satisfies it, in the order in which Form H asks for it. Flag any Regulation 38 requirement the structure does not yet address.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
