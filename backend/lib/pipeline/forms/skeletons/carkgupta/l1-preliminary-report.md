# L1: Preliminary Report
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 13, Liquidation Process Regulations`

L1   Preliminary Report

Regulation 13, Liquidation Process Regulations

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The contents and the period within which the report is to be submitted.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the report.



WHY IT MATTERS

It is the liquidator's first statement to the Adjudicating Authority of what the estate actually contains and whether the process is likely to be funded.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft a preliminary report under Regulation 13. Cover the capital structure, the estimated assets and liabilities as at commencement, whether further inquiry into any matter is required, the proposed plan of action with timelines, and the estimated liquidation cost with the proposed source of funding.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Capital structure of the corporate debtor

Estimated assets and liabilities at commencement

Basis on which the estimates have been drawn

Whether further inquiry is required, and into what

Proposed plan of action with a timeline

Estimated liquidation cost and the funding source

Assets not traceable or not in possession

Pending litigation and its effect on realisation

Date of submission and acknowledgement
