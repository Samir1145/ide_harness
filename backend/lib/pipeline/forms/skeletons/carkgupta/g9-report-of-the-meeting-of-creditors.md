# G9: Report of the Meeting of Creditors
**Statutory Authority:** `Sections 111 to 114`

G9   Report of the Meeting of Creditors

Sections 111 to 114

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The convening of the meeting, the voting rights and the majority required.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the report of the meeting, nor for the record of modifications to the plan agreed at the meeting.



WHY IT MATTERS

Modifications agreed at the meeting change the plan that the Adjudicating Authority is asked to approve. If they are not recorded precisely, the approved plan is uncertain.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional under Section 114. Draft the report of the meeting of creditors, recording attendance with voting shares, the plan as placed and the plan as modified, the modifications agreed with the debtor's consent, the voting result against the statutory majority, and whether the plan stands approved.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.

Whether the plan stands approved
