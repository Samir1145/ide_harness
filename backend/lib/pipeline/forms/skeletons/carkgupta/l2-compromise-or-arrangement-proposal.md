# L2: Compromise or Arrangement Proposal
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 2B | Section 230, Companies Act 2013`

L2   Compromise or Arrangement Proposal

Regulation 2B | Section 230, Companies Act 2013

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The window within which a compromise or arrangement may be proposed, and who may not propose one.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the proposal, for the liquidator's report on it, or for the record of consultation.



WHY IT MATTERS

The route is time-barred and the eligibility bar is strict. A proposal that arrives without structure consumes the whole window.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft the structure of a proposal for a compromise or arrangement under Regulation 2B read with Section 230 of the Companies Act 2013, and the liquidator's report on it. Address the eligibility of the proposer, the time available within the window, the treatment of each class of stakeholder, and the effect on the liquidation if the proposal fails.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Identity of the proposer and eligibility check

Date of the liquidation order and the window remaining

Class-wise treatment of stakeholders

Comparison with the expected liquidation realisation

Funding, sources and the implementation schedule

Liquidator's report and recommendation

Record of consultation with the SCC

Application to the Bench and the reliefs sought

Consequence if the proposal is not sanctioned
