# G3: Appointment and Consent Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Sections 97, 98`

G3   Appointment and Consent Record

Sections 97, 98

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The mechanism for the appointment of the resolution professional and the circumstances of replacement.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the consent, the disclosure of independence, or the record of the professional's own eligibility.



WHY IT MATTERS

A challenge to the professional's appointment derails the entire Part III process at the outset, and the answer to it is the record made at appointment.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a professional accepting an appointment under Section 97. Draft a consent and disclosure record covering eligibility, the absence of any disciplinary proceeding, relationships with the debtor, the creditors and their advisers, prior engagements, and a positive statement of independence. Include the date of consent and the date of the order.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Reference to the application and the order

Consent to act, with the date

Registration and authorisation particulars

Disciplinary proceedings, if any, disclosed

Relationships with the debtor and the creditors

Prior engagements touching the same debt

Positive statement of independence

Fee proposed and the basis of it

Disclosure to the Board and to the agency
