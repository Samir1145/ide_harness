# P7: Preliminary Information Memorandum
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 54G`

P7   Preliminary Information Memorandum

Section 54G

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The contents, and the period within which the corporate debtor must submit it.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format, and no format for the resolution professional's own note on its adequacy.



WHY IT MATTERS

The memorandum is prepared by the corporate debtor and relied on by the committee. Where the professional does not record his assessment of it, he adopts it by silence.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional in a pre-packaged process. Draft the structure of a preliminary information memorandum under Section 54G, mapping each statutory requirement to a numbered chapter, together with the resolution professional's separate note recording what has been verified, what has not, and what the committee should treat with caution.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
