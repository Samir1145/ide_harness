# G5: List of Creditors under Section 104
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 104`

G5   List of Creditors under Section 104

Section 104

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The obligation to prepare a list of creditors on the basis of the information in the application and the claims received.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the list, and none for the reconciliation between the claims received and the debtor's own disclosure.



WHY IT MATTERS

The difference between what the debtor disclosed and what the creditors claim is the single most informative document in a Part III process, and it is nobody's prescribed obligation to produce it.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional under Section 104. Draft a list of creditors together with a reconciliation between the debts disclosed by the debtor in the application and the claims actually received. Show, creditor by creditor, the amount disclosed, the amount claimed, the difference and the explanation. Flag every creditor who claims but was not disclosed.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
