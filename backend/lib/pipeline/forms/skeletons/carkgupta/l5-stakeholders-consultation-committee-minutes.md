# L5: Stakeholders' Consultation Committee Minutes
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 31A`

L5   Stakeholders' Consultation Committee Minutes

Regulation 31A

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

Constitution, representation and the matters on which the committee is to be consulted.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the notice, the minutes or the record of the liquidator's reasons where the advice is not accepted.



WHY IT MATTERS

Where the liquidator departs from the advice of the committee, the reasons must be recorded contemporaneously. That record has no prescribed form and is very often simply absent.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft the minutes of a meeting of the stakeholders' consultation committee under Regulation 31A. Record the composition and voting shares, the matters placed for advice, the advice tendered with the percentage supporting it, and separately, where the liquidator proposes not to accept the advice, the reasons recorded in writing.
