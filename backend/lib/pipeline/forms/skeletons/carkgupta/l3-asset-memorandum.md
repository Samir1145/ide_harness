# L3: Asset Memorandum
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 34`

L3   Asset Memorandum

Regulation 34

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The contents, and the restriction on sharing it without permission.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format.



WHY IT MATTERS

It carries the valuation of every asset and is the reference against which every sale is judged afterwards.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft an asset memorandum under Regulation 34. For each asset show a description, the book value, the valuation obtained with the valuer named, the intended manner and mode of sale, the expected realisation, and any encumbrance or third-party claim. Add a note on assets not traceable and on assets excluded from the liquidation estate.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Asset register — description, location, identification

Book value, and the valuation with the valuer named

Encumbrances, security interest and third-party claims

Intended manner, mode and timing of sale

Expected realisation and the reserve price basis

Assets not traceable, with the steps taken

Assets excluded from the liquidation estate

Intangibles, contractual rights and receivables

Restriction on sharing without leave of the Bench
