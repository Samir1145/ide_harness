# C7: Voting Record and e-Voting Result Sheet
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulations 25, 25(5), 26`

C7   Voting Record and e-Voting Result Sheet

Regulations 25, 25(5), 26

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The obligation to provide for electronic voting and the windows within which votes are to be cast.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the result sheet recording, member by member, how each voting share was cast on each resolution.



WHY IT MATTERS

The commercial wisdom of the committee is only as provable as the voting record behind it.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a resolution professional. Draft an e-voting result sheet for a meeting of the committee of creditors. Show each resolution, the voting window with opening and closing timestamps, each member with voting share, the vote cast, abstentions and non-participation, the percentage in favour, and whether the resolution stands carried against the applicable threshold. Cite the threshold provision for each item.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Meeting reference and the resolutions put to vote

e-voting platform and the window with timestamps

Member-wise table: name | share | vote | timestamp

Members not voting, and treatment of their shares

Authorised representative votes for a class

Percentage in favour and against for each resolution

Applicable threshold and the provision under which it arises

Result declared — carried or not carried

Certificate of the scrutiniser or the RP
