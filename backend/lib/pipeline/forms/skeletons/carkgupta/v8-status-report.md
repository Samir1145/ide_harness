# V8: Status Report
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 35`

V8   Status Report

Regulation 35

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The periodicity and the contents of the status report to the contributories.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format.



WHY IT MATTERS

Voluntary liquidations that overrun their timeline are almost always ones where the status reports stopped being written.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a voluntary liquidation liquidator. Draft a status report to the contributories under Regulation 35, carrying forward the previous period's closing position, and covering realisation, claims admitted, distribution, statutory clearances outstanding, liquidation cost, and the revised expected date of dissolution with reasons for any change.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Period, and the opening position carried forward

Assets realised in the period and cumulatively

Claims received, admitted and rejected

Distribution to stakeholders and the balance held

Statutory clearances applied for and outstanding

Liquidation cost incurred and paid

Bank accounts and the closing balance

Revised expected date of dissolution

Reasons for any change in the timeline
