# V3: Proof of Public Announcement
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 14, Voluntary Liquidation Regulations`

V3   Proof of Public Announcement

Regulation 14, Voluntary Liquidation Regulations

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The form of the public announcement and the media in which it is to be published.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the liquidator's record proving publication in each required medium.



WHY IT MATTERS

In a solvent liquidation the announcement is the only notice most creditors will ever receive. Its proof is what answers a creditor who surfaces after dissolution.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a voluntary liquidation liquidator. Prepare a proof of publication record for the public announcement under Regulation 14, capturing each newspaper with its edition, language, date and page, the company's website with the upload date, and the intimation to the Board. Attach an annexure index for the tear sheets.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
