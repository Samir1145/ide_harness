# V2: Declaration of Solvency
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 59(3)(a)`

V2   Declaration of Solvency

Section 59(3)(a)

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

That a majority of directors shall make a declaration verified by an affidavit, accompanied by audited financials and a valuation of assets.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the declaration itself, and none for the record that the accompanying documents were in fact annexed.



WHY IT MATTERS

A defective declaration puts the entire voluntary liquidation at risk, and the defect is usually discovered only at the dissolution stage.

PROMPT

Act as counsel advising the directors of a company proposing voluntary liquidation. Draft a declaration of solvency under Section 59(3)(a), verified by affidavit, stating full inquiry into the affairs, the opinion on ability to pay debts in full, and that the liquidation is not to defraud any person. Attach an annexure index for the audited financial statements and the valuation report.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
