# L9: Distribution Statement under Section 53
**Statutory Authority:** `Section 53 | Regulations 42, 43`

L9   Distribution Statement under Section 53

Section 53 | Regulations 42, 43

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The order of priority, and the period within which the proceeds are to be distributed.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the distribution statement showing the waterfall computation, class by class, with the working behind each figure.



WHY IT MATTERS

The waterfall is the most frequently challenged arithmetic in any liquidation. Where the computation is not shown as a working, it cannot be defended two years later.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a liquidator. Draft a distribution statement under Section 53. Show the amount available, the deductions for liquidation cost, and then class by class in the statutory order: the amount due, the amount distributed, the percentage recovery and the shortfall. Show the working for the pari passu computation within each class. Cite the clause of Section 53 against each class.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.
