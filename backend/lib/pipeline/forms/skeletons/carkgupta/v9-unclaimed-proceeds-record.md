# V9: Unclaimed Proceeds Record
**Statutory Authority:** `Regulation 39, Voluntary Liquidation Regulations`

V9   Unclaimed Proceeds Record

Regulation 39, Voluntary Liquidation Regulations

WHAT THE CODE PRESCRIBES

The obligation to deposit unclaimed proceeds into the Corporate Voluntary Liquidation Account.

WHAT IT DOES NOT PRESCRIBE

No format for the record correlating each unclaimed amount to the stakeholder and the attempts made to pay it.



WHY IT MATTERS

A solvent company's stakeholder who did not collect payment may return long after dissolution. The record is the only thing that will identify the entitlement.

PROMPT

Act as counsel to a voluntary liquidation liquidator. Draft a record of unclaimed proceeds for deposit into the Corporate Voluntary Liquidation Account. For each amount show the stakeholder, the class, the amount, the date it fell due, the attempts made to pay, the reason it remains unclaimed and the last known particulars.

Attach the bare provision to the project before running this. Verify every citation before use.



DRAFT TEMPLATE — HEADS TO CARRY

Total unclaimed proceeds and the composition

Stakeholder-wise: class, amount, date due

Attempts made to effect payment, with dates

Reason the amount remains unclaimed

Last known particulars of the stakeholder

Date of deposit into the Account

Statement accompanying the deposit

Reconciliation to the final report
