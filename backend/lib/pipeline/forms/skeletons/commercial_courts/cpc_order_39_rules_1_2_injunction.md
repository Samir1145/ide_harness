**MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:**

1. **Institution of Commercial Suit:**  
   That the Plaintiff has instituted the accompanying Commercial Suit seeking permanent injunction, specific performance, and monetary recovery against the Defendants in respect of commercial arrangements concerning **{{SCHEDULE_PROPERTY_DESCRIPTION}}** (hereinafter referred to as the "Suit Property"). The contents of the accompanying Plaint may be read as an integral part and parcel of the present application.

2. **Uncontroverted Rights & Breach by Defendants:**  
   That under the registered / executed contract dated {{AGREEMENT_DATE}}, the Plaintiff acquired valid, subsisting, and legally protected commercial rights in respect of the Suit Property. The Defendants, in gross violation of contractual covenants and statutory obligations, are attempting to defeat the rights of the Plaintiff by orchestrating clandestine transfers and disturbing peaceful commercial possession / status quo.

3. **Three-Fold Judicial Test for Grant of Temporary Injunction:**  
   * **A. Strong Prima Facie Case:**  
     The Plaintiff has established an unassailable legal right based on registered instruments, undisputed email correspondence, and verified banking transactions. There is a serious triable issue with a high probability of ultimate success in favor of the Plaintiff.
   * **B. Overwhelming Balance of Convenience:**  
     The balance of convenience lies entirely in favor of maintaining the status quo of the Suit Property. An order of injunction preserves the subject matter in dispute during the pendency of trial. In stark contrast, if no restraint is granted, the Defendants will create irreversible third-party encumbrances, entangling innocent purchasers and multiplying litigation.
   * **C. Irreparable Injury & Financial Loss:**  
     If the Defendants succeed in alienating, parting with possession, or modifying the physical character of the Suit Property, the injury inflicted upon the Plaintiff will be absolute, irreparable, and incapable of being compensated by monetary damages.

4. **Necessity of Ad-Interim Ex-Parte Relief:**  
   That the Defendants are taking frantic and surreptitious steps to execute deeds of transfer and part with possession. If advance notice of the present application is given, the very purpose of seeking urgent protection will be completely defeated by the Defendants acting with speed to create faits accomplis before the matter is heard.

5. **Mandatory Compliance with Order XXXIX Rule 3 CPC Proviso:**  
   The Plaintiff hereby gives a solemn undertaking to this Hon'ble Court that immediately upon the grant of ad-interim ex-parte injunction, the Plaintiff shall deliver to the Defendants / dispatch by registered post / speed post and email within 24 hours copies of the application, plaint, affidavit, and all documents relied upon, and shall file an affidavit of compliance on the very next working day.
