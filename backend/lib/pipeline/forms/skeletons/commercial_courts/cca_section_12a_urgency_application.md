1. **Institution of Commercial Suit with Urgent Interim Relief:**  
   That the Plaintiff has instituted the accompanying Commercial Suit against the Defendants seeking substantial monetary recovery of **₹{{DISPUTED_QUANTUM}}/-**, permanent injunction, and protective interim relief under Order XXXVIII Rule 5 and/or Order XXXIX Rules 1 & 2 CPC.

2. **Statutory Framework under Section 12A of Commercial Courts Act, 2015:**  
   That Section 12A(1) of the Commercial Courts Act, 2015 provides as under:
   > *"12A. Pre-Institution Mediation and Settlement.—(1) A suit, which does not contemplate any urgent interim relief under this Act, shall not be instituted unless the plaintiff exhausts the remedy of pre-institution mediation in accordance with such manner and procedure as may be prescribed by rules made by the Central Government."*

3. **Judicial Dictum in *Patil Automation* & Scope of the Proviso:**  
   That the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in *Patil Automation Private Limited v. Rakheja Engineers Private Limited, (2022) 10 SCC 1*, held that the mandate of Section 12A is mandatory in nature, except where the suit contemplates urgent interim relief. The statutory scheme explicitly carves out suits where immediate interim intervention of the Court is necessary to protect the subject matter of the dispute or prevent irreversible fraud.

   That in the present case, urgent interim relief is actively contemplated and sought by the Plaintiff via accompanying interlocutory applications:
   * **A. Active Dissipation of Assets (Order XXXVIII Rule 5 CPC):** The Defendants are attempting to alienate, mortgage, and transfer their sole unencumbered immovable property bearing **{{SCHEDULE_PROPERTY_DESCRIPTION}}** to defeat the execution of any decree.
   * **B. Threat to Alter Status Quo (Order XXXIX Rules 1 & 2 CPC):** The Defendants are threatening to alter the physical status, induct third-party transferees, and create encumbrances over the Suit Property.
   * **C. Inadequacy of Mediation in Pre-empting Fraud:** If the Plaintiff is relegated to pre-institution mediation before the Legal Services Authority (which statutorily takes up to 3 to 5 months under Section 12A(3)), the Defendants will succeed in alienating the properties and stripping all assets, rendering the entire suit infructuous.

5. **Bona Fide Urgency:**  
   The prayer for urgent interim relief is genuine, urgent, and substantiated by contemporaneous documents on record, and is not a pretext to bypass statutory mediation.
