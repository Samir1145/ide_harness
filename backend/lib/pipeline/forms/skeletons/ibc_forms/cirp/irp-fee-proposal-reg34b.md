## 1. STATUTORY BACKGROUND & REGULATORY MANDATE

1. In accordance with **Section 20 and Section 28** of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 (IBC), the Interim Resolution Professional (IRP) is mandated to manage the operations of the Corporate Debtor as a going concern and preserve the value of its estate.
2. In terms of **Regulation 33 & 34 of the CIRP Regulations, 2016**:
   - The applicant financial creditor or the Committee of Creditors (CoC) shall fix the expenses to be incurred by the IRP.
   - The expenses incurred by the IRP in connection with the CIRP until the constitution of the CoC shall be ratified by the CoC at its first meeting and reimbursed.
3. In terms of **Regulation 34B and Schedule-II** (as amended by IBBI), the fee payable to an IRP/RP shall be in accordance with the statutory fee structure linked to the quantum of admitted claims and turnover brackets, or as mutually agreed and ratified by the CoC.

## 4. PRIORITY OF REPAYMENT (§ 53(1)(a) READ WITH § 30(2)(a))

The Committee of Creditors is requested to note that pursuant to **Section 53(1)(a) and Section 30(2)(a)** of the IBC, 2016:
> *"The Insolvency Resolution Process Costs (IRPC) shall be paid in priority to all other debts in full before any disbursement is made to financial or operational creditors."*

Any interim finance or financial bridge raised to fund this budget shall rank as paramount super-priority operational debt.
