Encrypted In-Memory Law Vaults

Hayagriva bundles 4,026+ statutory bare act sections and over 17,500+ court judgments directly into offline, AES-256 encrypted vaults. You can cite laws and precedents in Monaco with sub-millisecond autocompletion even completely disconnected from the internet.

Primary Drafting Shortcuts in Monaco Editor
Trigger Vault Queried Description & Example
@@ or @sec Statutory Law Vault Search 4,026+ bare act provisions. Type @@moratorium or @sec 14 to insert verbatim statutory text.
@ibc/ Insolvency Code Direct IBC provision filter. Type @ibc/29a or @ibc/43 for section text and explanations.
@mca/ Companies Act Corporate statutory filter. Type @mca/188 for related party rules.
@precedent/ Court Precedents Query 17,500+ rulings. Type @precedent/ruchi soya to insert judgment title, tribunal, and ratio.
/clause Clause Boilerplate Notion-style interactive picker. Inserts Arbitration, Moratorium, Severability, Force Majeure, or Confidentiality clauses.
/fact Case Fact Dictionary Insert verified variables: Corporate Debtor, Total Admitted Debt, IRP Name, Admission Date.
/export Supreme Court DOCX Deterministic compiler conforming to Supreme Court rules (A4, 14pt Times New Roman, 1.5 spacing, 4cm margins).
/export-pdf Court PDF Compiles active Markdown into court-ready formatted PDF and opens live preview.
Statutory Hover Providers

When reviewing or drafting Markdown documents, hover your mouse cursor over any legal citation:

Pursuant to Section 14 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code...

Monaco immediately displays a rich tooltip with the complete bare act provision, statutory amendments, and operational explanations without switching tabs.

Interactive Citation Deep-Links

Link companion Markdown notes directly to source PDF dossier pages using double brackets:

[[Sanction_Letter_State_Bank.pdf#page=4]]

Clicking this link automatically opens the source PDF in the middle viewer and navigates directly to Page 4 side-by-side with your active Markdown draft.
