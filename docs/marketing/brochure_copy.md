## Page 2: VISION & THE THREE-STEP PIPELINE
*   **Header (Outfit, Bold, 20pt):**  
    Cases are Code. Treat Them Like It.
*   **Column 1: The Vision**  
    Legal operations are buried under context fragmentation. Lawyers spend 70% of their billable hours navigating hundreds of pages of unstructured PDFs, searching for dates, verifying invoices, and tracking limitation periods.
    
    HAYAGRIVA transforms case management by introducing a **Legal Compiler Toolchain**. Just as developers write, compile, and debug code, legal teams can ingest documents, verify facts in Monaco, and execute specialized audit agents on a unified case schema.

*   **Column 2: The Three-Step Pipeline**
    -   **Step 1 — Ingest & Convert (Excavate):** Automatically parses PDFs, Word files, Excel files, and wikis into companion Markdown format. Extracts text locally, bypassing scanned files, and calculates vector search embeddings offline.
    -   **Step 2 — Curation & Verify (HIL Enrichment):** Edit facts in the Monaco editor. Updates are synced automatically to a SQLite database. Autocomplete references using decrypted statute vaults, and inspect timelines or topic overlaps.
    -   **Step 3 — Action & Audit (Agent Outputs):** Spawn specialized legal agents to verify claims, scan ledgers for preferential transfers, score resolution plans, or compile compliance templates.

## Page 3: SPECIALIZED AUDIT AGENTS
*   **Header (Outfit, Bold, 20pt):**  
    Run Audits on Tap. Let Agents Do the Legwork.
*   **Agent Feature Showreel:**
    -   **Avoidance Scanner Agent:** Audits ledger tables, cash flows, and bank accounts. Flags suspicious transactions (preferential, undervalued, fraudulent) with clear statutory citations.
    -   **Claims Verification Agent:** Cross-references financial creditor invoices, security records, and statutory claims tables. Identifies validation gaps and prints evaluation card summaries.
    -   **Resolution Plan Evaluator:** Audits competing resolution plans against statutory compliance sheets. Renders scoring matrices.
    -   **NCLT Drafter Agent:** Compiles draft skeletons for applications and petitions. Unlocks structured legal template drafting while preserving user placeholders.
    -   **Litigation Tracker Agent:** Scans case files to track limitation periods, statute thresholds, and case chronology histories.
