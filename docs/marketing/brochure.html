HAYAGRIVA

Legal Compiler & IDE

A local-first, privacy-guaranteed case analysis environment. Compile case files, synchronize facts to local databases, and run specialized legal audit agents.

PAGE 01 / COVER

Vision & Architecture

Legal workspaces are fundamentally fragmented. Lawyers must manually scroll through thousands of PDF pages, verify financial values across disparate spreadsheets, check statutory limitations, and draft structured briefs in isolated text editors.

HAYAGRIVA introduces the Legal Compiler. By treating case facts as unified variables and statutory records as index parameters, we allow teams to ingest documents, curate indices in a Monaco code environment, and compile final drafts deterministically.

Every parsed fact is stored inside local SQLite indices. Our core design guarantees that manual document updates sync automatically, allowing legal teams to focus on case strategy rather than database management.

STEP 1 Ingest & Convert

Raw files (PDFs, DOCX, XLSX) are converted locally to companion Markdown, while scanned data is automatically checked for density validation.

STEP 2 Verify & Enrich

Verify facts in the Monaco editor. Updates are saved instantly to SQLite. Search encrypted local legal vaults with auto-completes.

STEP 3 Action & Audit

Deploy pre-installed legal agents to scan transactions, audit claims, score competing plans, and compile draft skeletons.

PAGE 02 / VISION

Specialized Legal Agents

HAYAGRIVA comes pre-bundled with five specialized Node.js-native agents that run audits and generate structured checklists.

Avoidance Scanner Agent

Audits bank statements and transaction ledgers. Flags preferential, undervalued, or fraudulent transfers within statutory look-back frames.

Claims Verification Agent

Cross-references financial creditor claim submissions against corporate ledgers. Validates proof tables and outlines discrepancy records.

Resolution Plan Evaluator

Evaluates multiple resolution plans side-by-side against structural compliance checklists. Compiles final scoring matrices.

NCLT Drafter Agent

Generates ready-to-file applications and petitions. Maps compiled facts to specific templates while preserving unresolved gap placeholders.

Litigation Tracker Agent

Monitors litigation progress, records limitations timelines, indexes case precedents, and drafts periodic compliance reports.
