Insolvency Memo: Case Alpha

# 1. Background & CIRP Details

Alpha Steel Ltd. is a major steel manufacturing facility currently under corporate insolvency resolution process (CIRP). The CIRP commenced on January 15, 2025, following a petition filed under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). **Atul Grover has been appointed as the Insolvency Resolution Professional (IRP) to manage the operations and resolve outstanding claims.**

# 2. Initial Findings & Outstanding Claims

The IRP has collated claims from financial creditors, operational creditors, and employees. Initial findings indicate:

* Financial Creditors (Secured): INR 3,200 Cr admitted claims.
* Financial Creditors (Unsecured): INR 1,300 Cr admitted claims.
* Operational Creditors: INR 450 Cr admitted claims under verification.

# 3. Next Steps

The Committee of Creditors (CoC) will meet on July 10 to discuss resolution plan submissions and evaluate bidder qualifications.
