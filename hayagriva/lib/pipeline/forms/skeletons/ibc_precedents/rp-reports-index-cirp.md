__INDEX OF REPORTS, APPLICATIONS & PROCESS DOCUMENTS TO BE PREPARED BY THE RESOLUTION PROFESSIONAL IN CIRP__

__— for which no format is prescribed in the Code or the Regulations —__

__*Practice aid. *__*This index lists the documents that a Resolution Professional must author in a Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process where the Code and the IBBI (CIRP) Regulations, 2016 either specify the contents only, or are silent, and therefore prescribe no form or template — so that each must be drafted bespoke (on the lines of the firm’s Section 19(2) model application). Statutory and regulatory references are stated as amended up to 2026. Documents for which a form is prescribed are excluded and noted separately at the foot of this index.*

__A.  REPORTS, OPINIONS & CERTIFICATIONS (to the Adjudicating Authority / CoC / Board)__

__1__

__Report certifying constitution of the Committee of Creditors__

Sec. 21(1)–(2) r/w Reg. 17(1)

IRP certifies to the AA that the CoC has been duly constituted after collation and verification of claims; records its composition and voting shares. Drafted as a report (commonly on affidavit); no template prescribed.

Adjudicating Authority

Within 2 days of verification of claims (Reg. 17(1)); first CoC meeting within 7 days thereafter.

__2__

__Information Memorandum (IM)__

Sec. 29 r/w Reg. 36

Comprehensive document on the CD’s assets, liabilities, financials, litigation and related-party dealings, to enable resolution applicants to frame plans. Contents are specified in Reg. 36(2), but no form/template is prescribed.

CoC members; shared with PRAs on undertaking

On or before the 95th day of ICD (Reg. 36(1), as amended w.e.f. 18.09.2023), with subsequent updates (Fifth Amendment, 2025).

__3__

__Examination & confirmation of compliance of each resolution plan with Sec. 30(2)__

Sec. 30(2) r/w Reg. 39(2)–(3)

RP examines every plan for compliance (payment priorities, feasibility, statutory requirements, Sec. 29A eligibility) and places its confirmation/analysis before the CoC. No format — the later certificate to the AA is the prescribed Form H.

Committee of Creditors

Before the CoC considers/votes on plans; within the CIRP period.

__4__

__Opinion and Determination on avoidance / PUFE transactions__

Reg. 35A(1)–(2) r/w Secs. 43, 45, 47, 50, 66

RP forms an opinion whether the CD was subjected to preferential, undervalued, extortionate-credit, or fraudulent/wrongful-trading transactions, and then records a reasoned determination. No prescribed format.

Recorded by RP; basis for AA application & CoC disclosure

Opinion by 75th day; determination by 115th day of ICD.

__5__

__RP’s comments on the Transaction / Forensic Audit Report__

Sec. 25(2)(j) r/w Reg. 35A

Where a transaction auditor is appointed, the auditor’s report is placed before the CoC together with the RP’s comments. No format for the RP’s covering note/comments.

Committee of Creditors

On receipt of the audit report; before filing avoidance applications.

__6__

__Report on status of development rights & permissions (real-estate projects)__

Reg. 30C

For a CD with real-estate project(s), RP prepares a report on the status of development rights/permissions, places it before the CoC for comments, and submits it with those comments to the AA. No format.

CoC (for comments), then Adjudicating Authority

Within the timeline stipulated in Reg. 30C.

__7__

__Periodic progress / status reports to the CoC__

Secs. 23, 25 r/w Reg. 18

Periodic updates on the conduct of CIRP — claims position, cash flows, asset preservation, EOI/plan status and litigation. No prescribed format.

Committee of Creditors

Ongoing, through the CIRP.

__B.  APPLICATIONS TO THE ADJUDICATING AUTHORITY (no format prescribed)__

__8__

__Application against non-cooperating personnel / suspended management (with district-administration assistance)__

Sec. 19(2) r/w Sec. 60(5) and Reg. 30

Seeks directions compelling custody, control, records and cooperation, and police/district-administration assistance under Reg. 30. (Model draft already prepared by the firm.)

Adjudicating Authority

As and when non-cooperation arises.

__9__

__Application for extension of the CIRP period__

Sec. 12(3) r/w Reg. 40

On a CoC resolution (66%), seeks extension beyond 180 days, up to the 330-day outer limit. No format.

Adjudicating Authority

Before expiry of the 180-day period.

__10__

__Application for exclusion / extension of time__

Rule 11, NCLT Rules r/w Reg. 40C

Seeks exclusion of periods lost (litigation, stay, force majeure, handover delays) from the CIRP timeline. No format.

Adjudicating Authority

As and when required.

__11__

__Avoidance applications (preferential / undervalued / extortionate / fraudulent or wrongful trading)__

Secs. 43, 45, 47, 50, 66 r/w Reg. 35A(3)

Seeks reversal of impugned transactions or other appropriate relief, founded on the RP’s determination. No format.

Adjudicating Authority

By the 135th day of ICD (Reg. 35A(3)).

__12__

__Residuary / miscellaneous applications__

Sec. 60(5)

Recovery or protection of assets; directions to banks, authorities, the IU or third parties; replacement of valuers/professionals; any question of law or fact arising in the CIRP. No format.

Adjudicating Authority

As and when required.

__13__

__Application for approval of the resolution plan__

Sec. 30(6) r/w Sec. 31

RP submits the CoC-approved plan to the AA for approval. Accompanied by the prescribed Form H compliance certificate; the application itself has no format.

Adjudicating Authority

On CoC approval; within the CIRP period.

__14__

__Application for initiation of liquidation__

Sec. 33(1)/(2) r/w Reg. 40

Where no plan is received/approved within time, or the CoC resolves to liquidate, or an approved plan is contravened. No format.

Adjudicating Authority

On expiry of the CIRP / earlier CoC decision.

__15__

__Application placing the withdrawal request before the AA__

Sec. 12A r/w Reg. 30A

RP places the applicant’s withdrawal request before the AA after CoC approval (90%). Form FA governs the request itself; the RP’s covering application has no format.

Adjudicating Authority

On 90% CoC approval of withdrawal.

__C.  PROCESS & DEAL DOCUMENTS AUTHORED BY THE RP (no format prescribed)__

__16__

__Request for Resolution Plans (RFRP)__

Reg. 36B(1)

Detailed invitation/process document issued to PRAs setting out the manner of submission, timelines and terms of the plan. No format.

Prospective Resolution Applicants

After the provisional list of PRAs; within the CIRP timeline.

__17__

__Evaluation Matrix__

Sec. 5(ha) r/w Reg. 36B

Parameters, and the manner of applying them, as approved by the CoC, for comparison of resolution plans. No format.

Approved by CoC; issued to PRAs

With or before the RFRP / Form G.

__18__

__Minutes of the meetings of the Committee of Creditors__

Regs. 24 & 25

Record of CoC proceedings, voting and decisions; circulated to members and the Board. No prescribed format.

CoC members; the Board

Within 48 hours of each meeting.

__19__

__Custody / handover memorandum and final report on conclusion of CIRP__

Secs. 23, 25 (general)

Records handover of the CD, its records and assets to the successful resolution applicant on plan approval, or to the liquidator on liquidation, and closes out the RP’s stewardship. No format.

Resolution Applicant / Liquidator; CoC

On conclusion of the CIRP.

__DOCUMENTS EXCLUDED — FORMATS PRESCRIBED__

The following are excluded because IBBI has prescribed a form: Form A (public announcement, Reg. 6); Forms B / C / CA / D / E / F (claims, Regs. 7–9A); Form FA (withdrawal request, Reg. 30A); Form G (invitation for expression of interest, Reg. 36A); Form H (compliance certificate, Reg. 39(4)); and the CIRP Forms (CIRP-1 to CIRP-8) filed periodically with the Board (Reg. 40B / 40C).

__NOTE ON STATUTORY CURRENCY (2026)__

__•  Core position unchanged. __The duties and filings above flow from provisions and regulations that continue in force in substantially the same terms; none of them carries a prescribed form as on the date of this index.

__•  IBC (Amendment) Act, 2026. __Act No. 6 of 2026 received Presidential assent on 06.04.2026; its provisions come into force on such date(s) as the Central Government may notify, and several are not yet operative. Its principal reforms (admission timelines, the Creditor-Initiated Insolvency Resolution Process, restructured liquidation, strengthened avoidance provisions, and group/cross-border frameworks) do not introduce a format for any document in this index. Verify the notified commencement of any provision relied upon at the time of filing.

__•  CIRP Regulations & Forms. __The CIRP Regulations, 2016 have been amended through 2025–2026, and IBBI revised the CIRP Forms in May 2025. Before relying on this index, confirm that IBBI has not, in the interim, prescribed a form for any item listed above and use the current version of every excluded form.

__•  Verify before reliance. __This is a compiled practice aid, not legal advice; reconcile each entry against the bare Act, the current Regulations and the model CIRP timeline (Reg. 40A) before use.
