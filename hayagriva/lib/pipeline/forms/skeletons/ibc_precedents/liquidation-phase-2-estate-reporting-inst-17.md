__Instrument 17  —  Engagement letter__

__Engagement of two registered valuers__

__Statutory basis:  __*Regulation 35, IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016 — appointment of two registered valuers to determine the realisable value; SCC oversight of methodology and confidential sharing of valuation reports (2024 amendment).*

Where a valuation conducted during the CIRP is available and is not stale, the Liquidator may consider the average of those estimates. In other cases, or where a fresh valuation is required, the Liquidator shall, within seven days of the LCD, appoint two registered valuers as below.

To,  {{ THE_REGISTERED_VALUER }}, Reg. No. {{ IBBI_RV }}, Asset class: [Land & Building / Plant & Machinery / Securities or Financial Assets]

2. You shall independently submit your estimate; the average of the two registered valuers’ estimates will be taken as the value.
