**IBC Precedent Pack**

**Termination of Voluntary Liquidation Proceedings**

Section 59(5A)–(5C) of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 read
with Regulation 42 of the IBBI (Voluntary Liquidation Process)
Regulations, 2017

*For information and educational purposes only*

**A. Governing framework at a glance**

Until the 2026 amendments there was no route to exit a voluntary
liquidation once commenced. The termination mechanism, and the manner of
informing the Board (IBBI) and the Registrar of Companies (ROC), is now
as follows:

| **Instrument** | **Key provision** | **Effect** |
|----|----|----|
| IBC (Amendment) Act, 2026 | Inserts Section 59(5A), (5B) and (5C) | Creates the statutory power to terminate a voluntary liquidation before dissolution |
| IBBI (VLP) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026 — in force 01.06.2026 | Inserts Regulation 42 | Operationalises the mechanism; Reg. 42(3) requires intimation to the Board and the ROC within 7 days |
| Circular No. IBBI/VL/97/2026 dated 02.06.2026 | Notifies Form J | Prescribes “Intimation for Termination of Voluntary Liquidation Proceedings” (Form J), addressed to the Adjudicating Authority |

*Note on sequence: the Board and the ROC are informed through the
Regulation 42(3) notification (7-day timeline). Form J is the
Adjudicating Authority–facing intimation, and proof of the Regulation
42(3) notification is annexed to it.*

**F. Compliance flags**

1\. Timeline — Regulation 42(3): the intimation to the Board and the ROC
must be made within seven days, computed from the later of the special
resolution \[Sec. 59(5A)(a)\] or the creditor approval \[Sec.
59(5A)(b)\].

2\. Companies Act filing: the special resolution attracts Form MGT-14
under Section 117 of the Companies Act, 2013 within 30 days — a distinct
compliance from the Regulation 42(3) intimation.

3\. Mode: the Board leg is filed electronically on the Board’s platform;
the ROC leg is intimated to the Registrar as above.

4\. Consequences — Regulation 42(4): on termination the liquidator’s
appointment and term stand terminated, the liquidator ceases to exercise
any powers or functions under the Regulations, and no further action is
taken under the Regulations.

5\. Form J is addressed to the Adjudicating Authority; the Board and the
ROC are informed via the Regulation 42(3) notification, proof of which
is annexed to Form J.

6\. Verify the current Form J and its annexure lettering against
Circular No. IBBI/VL/97/2026 dated 02.06.2026 before use. Default place
/ jurisdiction: Delhi.
