*Under Regulation 31A of the IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016*

__A.  Basis and scope of this proforma__

*This is a model template for the internal records of a liquidator. It is not a statutory form. As on date the IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016 do not prescribe a format for SCC notices, agenda, attendance sheets or minutes. The earlier proforma may be used as a reference, with the composition and voting sections recast to mirror the present Committee-of-Creditors-style structure of the SCC. Adapt every bracketed placeholder to the facts of the matter.*

__Key features reflected in this template (verify against the current text of Regulation 31A before filing):__

1. Constitution: The SCC is constituted within 60 days of the Liquidation Commencement Date (LCD) from the list of stakeholders under Regulation 31. Until reconstitution, the Committee of Creditors formed during CIRP continues to function as the SCC.
2. First meeting: convened within 7 days of the LCD.
3. Voting share: each member's voting share is in proportion to its admitted claim to the total admitted claims — i.e. the CoC-style value-based model, not the earlier one-class-one-representative model.
4. No vote: a related party of the{{ CORPORATE_DEBTOR_NAME }}has no right to vote. Promoters, directors, partners and shareholders (or their representatives) may attend but do not vote.
5. Decisions / advice: an advice or decision of the SCC is carried by a vote of not less than 66% of the voting share present and voting; the SCC may likewise, by 66%, propose replacement of the liquidator (application in Form AA).
6. Convening on request: the liquidator convenes a meeting when he considers it necessary, and must convene one on a request of members holding at least 33% of the voting share.
7. Non-binding: under Regulation 31A(10) the advice is not binding; where the liquidator takes a decision different from the SCC's advice he records reasons and reports them (in the IBBI proforma) to the Adjudicating Authority and the Board, and in the next progress report.
8. Conduct of meetings: the provisions governing CoC meetings during CIRP apply, with the necessary modifications, to the conduct of SCC meetings.

*Transitional caution: the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (Amendment) framework of 2025–26 contemplates the Committee of Creditors directly supervising the liquidation process, which may in due course replace the SCC mechanism. Until the corresponding amendments to the Liquidation Regulations are notified, this proforma proceeds under the SCC framework of the existing Regulation 31A.*

__F.  Report of the Liquidator's Decision Differing from the Advice of the SCC__

*Under the proviso to sub-regulation (10) of Regulation 31A of the IBBI (Liquidation Process) Regulations, 2016*

*Where the liquidator takes a decision different from the advice given by the SCC, he records the reasons in writing and submits this report to the Adjudicating Authority and the Board within five days of the decision (on the electronic platform made available by the Board), and includes it in the next progress report. One report is used for each such decision.*
