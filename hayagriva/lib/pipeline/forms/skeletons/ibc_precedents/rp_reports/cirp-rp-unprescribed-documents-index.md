# INDEX OF REPORTS, APPLICATIONS & PROCESS DOCUMENTS PREPARED BY RESOLUTION PROFESSIONAL IN CIRP
*(For Documents Where No Form Is Prescribed in the Code or CIRP Regulations)*

### A. REPORTS, OPINIONS & CERTIFICATIONS

| Sl. | Document / Report Title | Governing Statutory Provision | Nature, Purpose & Key Contents | Submitted To / Target Authority | Indicative Statutory Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | **Report Certifying Constitution of CoC** | Sec. 21(1) r/w Reg. 17(1) | Certifies CoC constitution after claims collation & verification. | Adjudicating Authority (NCLT) | Within 2 Days of Claims Verification |
| 2. | **Information Memorandum (IM)** | Sec. 29 r/w Reg. 36 | Comprehensive document on CD assets, liabilities, and litigation. | CoC & PRAs (on undertaking) | On or before 95th Day of ICD |
| 3. | **Sec 30(2) Resolution Plan Examination Report** | Sec. 30(2) r/w Reg. 39 | Clause-wise statutory compliance examination of resolution plans. | Committee of Creditors | Prior to CoC Voting on Plans |
| 4. | **Avoidance / PUFE Opinion & Determination Record** | Reg. 35A r/w Secs 43, 45, 50, 66 | Reasoned determination on preferential, undervalued, extortionate, or fraudulent deals. | Recorded by RP / Shared with CoC | Opinion: 75th Day; Determination: 115th Day |
| 5. | **RP Comments on Forensic / Transaction Audit** | Sec. 25(2)(j) r/w Reg. 35A | RP's comments on findings of appointed transaction auditor. | Committee of Creditors | Upon Receipt of Audit Report |
| 6. | **Real-Estate Development Rights Report** | Reg. 30C | Status of land title, RERA registrations, and building approvals. | CoC (comments) & NCLT | As per Reg 30C Timeline |
| 7. | **Periodic CIRP Progress Reports** | Secs 23, 25 r/w Reg. 18 | Periodic updates on claims, cash flows, asset preservation, and litigation. | Committee of Creditors | Ongoing Throughout CIRP |

### B. APPLICATIONS TO ADJUDICATING AUTHORITY (NO FORM PRESCRIBED)

| Sl. | Application Type | Governing Provision | Nature & Purpose | Indicative Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. | **Non-Cooperation Application** | Sec. 19(2) r/w Reg. 30 | Seeks directions against suspended management & police assistance. | As and when non-cooperation occurs |
| 2. | **CIRP Period Extension Application** | Sec. 12(3) r/w Reg. 40 | Seeks extension beyond 180 days (up to 330 days) upon 66% CoC vote. | Prior to expiry of 180-day period |
| 3. | **Time Exclusion Application** | Rule 11 NCLT Rules r/w Reg. 40C | Seeks exclusion of periods lost due to litigation stays or handover delays. | As and when required |
| 4. | **Avoidance Applications (Sec 43/45/50/66)** | Secs 43, 45, 50, 66 r/w Reg. 35A(3) | Seeks reversal of transactions or restitution from directors. | On or before 135th Day of ICD |
