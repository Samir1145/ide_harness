# __Note — Effect of the Bankruptcy Application \(Section 124\) and the 2026 Amendment__

On the filing of an application for bankruptcy, Section 124 of the Code provides for the effect of the application, including an interim moratorium pending the bankruptcy order\. By the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code \(Amendment\) Act, 2026 \(in force 26 May 2026\), a new sub\-section has been inserted in Section 124, parallel to Section 96\(4\), to provide that the interim moratorium shall not be available where the application relates to a personal guarantor to a corporate debtor\. Section 121 \(application for bankruptcy\) has itself been amended by a linked change\.

*Accordingly, the bankruptcy application below does not press the protection of an interim moratorium in a personal\-guarantor matter\. To confirm before filing: the exact text and numbering of the amendments to Sections 121 and 124, and the current bankruptcy Forms following the Bankruptcy \(Second Amendment\) Regulations, 2026\.*

# __Drafting Notes__

•	These are template precedents for the bankruptcy stage of a personal\-guarantor matter under Chapter IV of Part III, from the bankruptcy application through the bankruptcy order, claims, the meeting of creditors, recovery of transactions defrauding creditors, distribution and discharge\.

•	Forum and roles: items A, E and G are filed before the NCLT New Delhi Bench; items B, C, D and F are issued by the Bankruptcy Trustee\. The bankruptcy application \(A\) also appears in shorter form at Precedent H of the post\-admission set\.

•	Interim moratorium: by the 2026 amendment to Section 124, the interim moratorium attaching to a bankruptcy application is not available in a personal\-guarantor matter; do not plead it \(mirror of Section 96\(4\) at the resolution stage\)\.

•	Transactions defrauding creditors: the new Section 164A gives the Bankruptcy Trustee a distinct application to restore the position and protect victims; it sits alongside the existing undervalued\-transaction \(Section 164\) and preference \(Section 165\) provisions\.

•	Distribution: the order of priority is under Section 178; the 2026 explanation clarifies the treatment of government dues\. Discharge is under Section 138, with the effect \(and excluded debts\) under Section 139\.

*Currency — to confirm against the gazette before filing: \(i\) the exact text of the amendments to Sections 121 and 124, of new Section 164A, and of the explanation to Section 178; \(ii\) the current bankruptcy Forms and fee following the Bankruptcy \(Second Amendment\) Regulations, 2026 \(notified 1 June 2026\); and \(iii\) Section 183A \(penalty for frivolous or vexatious proceedings\), as a risk note across Part III filings\. The Code\-level position reflects the IBC \(Amendment\) Act, 2026 in force 26 May 2026 \(MCA S\.O\. 2625\(E\) dated 22 May 2026\)\.*
