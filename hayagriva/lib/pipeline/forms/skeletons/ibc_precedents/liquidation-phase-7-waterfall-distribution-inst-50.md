__B\.  Rules applied__

- The liquidator’s fee is deducted proportionately from the proceeds payable to each class \(s\.53\(3\)\);
- At each stage, debts within a class are paid in full or, if proceeds are insufficient, in equal proportion \(pari passu\);
- Contractual arrangements between equal\-ranking recipients that disrupt the priority are disregarded, save an inter\-se arrangement between two secured creditors \(s\.53\(2\)\);
- “Workmen’s dues” carry the meaning in Section 326 of the Companies Act, 2013\.

__Note:  __*Government dues for the 2 years preceding the LCD fall under clause \(e\)\(i\); the balance falls under clause \(f\)\. The treatment of statutory charges as a “secured creditor” \(Rainbow Papers line\) is contested — follow the statute and the latest binding authority for the matter\.*
