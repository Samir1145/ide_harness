__A.  Legal basis — which undertaking, under which provision__

*These are model undertakings to be obtained by the resolution professional before sharing the Information Memorandum (IM) and relevant information. They are given by the recipient (the CoC member or the resolution applicant) in favour of the resolution professional. Adapt every bracketed placeholder to the facts of the matter.*

__Recipient__

__Source provision__

__Effect__

Member of the CoC

Regulation 36(4), CIRP Regulations

The RP shares the IM only after receiving an undertaking that the member will keep the information confidential, not use it to cause undue gain or loss to itself or any other person, and comply with Section 29(2).

Prospective / Resolution Applicant

Section 29(2) of the Code, read with Regulation 36A(8)(g)

Access to relevant information is given only against the applicant's undertaking on confidentiality and insider trading, protection of the{{ CORPORATE_DEBTOR_NAME }}'s IP, and no third-party sharing; the same undertaking accompanies the expression of interest.

*Note on numbering: the CoC-member undertaking is under Regulation 36(4) — Regulation 36A is the invitation for expression of interest, and the applicant's confidentiality undertaking sits in Regulation 36A(8)(g) and in Section 29(2). A separate undertaking for the fair value / liquidation value of the{{ CORPORATE_DEBTOR_NAME }}is dealt with under Regulation 35(2) and is not covered here.*
