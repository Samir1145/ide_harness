__B. Bankruptcy forms for personal guarantors__

Circular — Formats under the IBBI (Bankruptcy Process for Personal Guarantors to Corporate Debtors) Regulations, 2019, dated 02.06.2026 (read with the 2026 Amendment Regulations). The 2026 amendment rationalised the bankruptcy forms and introduced coordination between the bankruptcy trustee and the corporate debtor's resolution professional for asset transfers.

The bankruptcy process (Chapter IV, Part III of the Code) covers, in outline: written consent to act as bankruptcy trustee (regulation 4); claim with proof by a creditor (regulation 7); and the proxy form for meetings of creditors (regulation 21). For the exact, current formats, refer to the 02.06.2026 bankruptcy-formats circular on the IBBI website.
