__A.  CIRP — Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process__

__Form A — Public Announcement__

*Reg 6*

☐  NCLT order admitting the CIRP application (with date of admission).

☐  Record of IRP appointment; valid Authorisation for Assignment (AFA).

☐  Corporate details: CIN / LLPIN, date and authority of incorporation, registered and principal office address.

☐  Insolvency commencement date and the computed last date for claims (14 days from IRP appointment).

☐  Estimated date of closure of the CIRP.

☐  Identification of any classes of creditors under section 21(6A)(b).

☐  Three IP names identified per class, each with a Form AB consent.

☐  Correspondence e-mail set up for the process.

☐  Newspaper(s) for publication — one English and one regional, at the location of the registered / principal office.

__Forms AA / AB — Written Consent (RP / Authorised Representative)__

*Reg 3(1A),(1B) / 4A(3)*

☐  IP registration certificate with the Board.

☐  Valid AFA and its date of expiry.

☐  IPA membership in good standing (no pending disciplinary proceedings).

☐  Statement of processes in hand (roles and numbers).

☐  The CoC resolution / IRP proposal referencing the proposed appointment.

__Form AC — List of Assets and Records__

*Reg 3A*

☐  Records of information under section 18(a): books of account, statutory registers, agreements, IT / GST records, etc.

☐  Asset list per the balance sheet / records (section 18(f)) with book values, locations and encumbrances.

☐  Handover memorandum / inventory taken while assuming custody and control.

☐  Identity and signatures of the person handing over (section 19) and of two witnesses.

☐  Section 19 requisition notices, where any records or assets are not handed over.

__Forms B / C / CA / D / E / F — Proof / Submission of Claim__

*Reg 7 / 8 / 8A / 9 / 9A*

☐  Proof of debt: invoices, ledgers, loan / facility agreements, sanction letters, statements of account.

☐  Computation of the claim amount with interest up to the insolvency commencement date.

☐  Security / charge documents (CERSAI record, ROC Form CHG, mortgage / hypothecation deeds), where secured.

☐  Identity / constitution proof: PAN / Aadhaar (individual); CIN and incorporation proof; GSTIN; partner IDs.

☐  Board resolution / authority letter for the person signing the claim.

☐  Bank account proof for transfer (cancelled cheque / bank certificate).

☐  Details of any set-off / mutual dealings, disputes, or pending suit / arbitration.

☐  For class creditors (Form CA): the chosen Authorised Representative.

☐  For workmen / employees (Forms D / E): employment records, wage registers, and the AR's authorisation (Form E).

__Form FA — Application for Withdrawal of CIRP (section 12A)__

*Reg 30A*

☐  CoC minutes approving withdrawal with at least 90% voting share.

☐  Copy of the application (diary / CP number) admitted by the AA.

☐  Bank guarantee / demand draft towards CIRP costs (Reg 30A).

☐  Settlement terms and proof of settlement with the applicant.

__Form G — Invitation for Expression of Interest__

*Reg 36A(1)*

☐  Corporate details, PAN / CIN, website, plant / asset locations, installed capacity.

☐  Last two years' financial statements with schedules; list of creditors (URL).

☐  Eligibility criteria for resolution applicants under section 25(2)(h).

☐  Timeline dates: provisional / final PRA list, IM, request for resolution plans, plan submission.

☐  MSME registration status; process e-mail id.

__Form GA — Application for Two-Stage Approval of Resolution Plan__

*Reg 39(3C)*

☐  The CoC-approved resolution plan.

☐  Compliance certificate (Form H).

☐  CoC minutes: approval of the plan, and approval for the two-stage process (proviso to section 31(1)).

☐  Reasons for two-stage approval and the proposed manner of implementation / distribution.

__Form H — Compliance Certificate__

*Reg 39(4)*

☐  The final CoC-approved resolution plan.

☐  SRA's section 29A eligibility affidavit and the RP's due-diligence certificate.

☐  Section 30(1) affidavit by the resolution applicant.

☐  CoC meeting minutes and voting records (with voting %).

☐  Fair value and liquidation value (registered valuers' reports).

☐  Performance guarantee details and validity.

☐  PUFE application particulars and AA orders, if any.

☐  MSME registration (if applicable); list of approvals required with timelines.

☐  Regulation 39B contribution computation; regulatory-fee (Reg 31A) affidavit.

☐  Details of pending IAs and income-tax carried-forward losses.

__B.  Liquidation Process__

__Form AA — Written Consent to Act as Liquidator__

*Reg 8B*

☐  IP registration certificate and valid AFA.

☐  Confirmation of no pending disciplinary proceedings.

☐  Confirmation that the liquidator did not act as RP of the corporate debtor.

☐  Statement of processes in hand.

__Form B — Public Announcement__

*Reg 12*

☐  Liquidation order under section 33.

☐  Handover of charge from the RP to the liquidator.

☐  Corporate details of the CD.

☐  Newspaper(s) for publication.

__Form C — Progress Report (Quarterly)__

*Reg 15*

☐  Minutes of Stakeholders' Consultation Committee (SCC) meetings.

☐  Asset memorandum; sale reports; registered-valuer reports.

☐  Receipts and payments for the quarter and cumulatively.

☐  Status of material litigation and of PUFE applications.

☐  Details of professionals engaged (fee, tenure, work).

☐  Revised estimate of liquidation cost, if any.

__Form G — Intimation of Decision on Security Interest__

*Reg 21A*

☐  Security creation documents.

☐  CERSAI record / ROC charge ID.

☐  Board resolution / authorisation of the secured creditor.

☐  Admitted claim particulars and valuation of the secured asset(s).

__Form H — Compliance Certificate__

*Reg 45*

☐  Asset memorandum and final sale report.

☐  Distribution records as per the section 53 waterfall.

☐  SCC minutes; registered-valuer reports.

☐  Progress reports and audited receipts and payments.

☐  Statutory intimations (PF / ESI / Income-tax / GST).

☐  Proof of deposits into the Corporate Liquidation Account.

☐  PUFE application particulars; Regulation 47 timeline.

__Form I — Deposit of Unclaimed Dividends / Undistributed Proceeds__

*Reg 46(5)*

☐  Stakeholder-wise entitlement list.

☐  TDS computation for each amount.

☐  Bank acknowledgement of deposit into the Corporate Liquidation Account.

__Form J — Withdrawal from Corporate Liquidation Account__

*Reg 46(7),(7D)*

☐  Dissolution order.

☐  Proof of the stakeholder's admitted claim.

☐  Stakeholder identity documents.

☐  Bank account details for transfer.

__C.  Voluntary Liquidation Process__

__Form A — Public Announcement__

*Reg 14*

☐  Declaration of solvency by a majority of directors / designated partners.

☐  Members' special resolution (or partners' resolution) for VL and appointment of liquidator.

☐  Creditors' approval (two-thirds in value of debt), where the company owes any debt.

☐  Intimation to the Registrar of Companies and the Board.

☐  Newspaper(s) for publication.

__Forms B / C / D / E / F — Proof of Claim (affidavit-based)__

*Reg 16 / 17 / 18(1) / 18(2) / 19*

☐  Proof of debt and computation with interest up to the liquidation commencement date.

☐  Identity / constitution proof and bank account details.

☐  Details of any set-off / mutual dealings.

☐  Affidavit sworn before a notary / oath commissioner, with verification.

__Form G — Deposit of Unclaimed Dividends / Undistributed Proceeds__

*Reg 39(5)*

☐  Stakeholder-wise entitlement list.

☐  TDS computation.

☐  Bank acknowledgement of deposit into the Corporate VL Account.

__Form H — Compliance Certificate__

*Reg 38(3)*

☐  Declaration of solvency and the members' / partners' resolution.

☐  Preliminary report and final report.

☐  Audited receipts and payments since the LCD.

☐  Distribution records (sections 52 / 53).

☐  Timeline compliance under Regulation 37.

__Form I — Withdrawal from Corporate Voluntary Liquidation Account__

*Reg 39(7)*

☐  Dissolution order.

☐  Proof of the stakeholder's admitted claim.

☐  Stakeholder identity documents.

☐  Bank account details for transfer.

__Form J — Intimation for Termination of VL Proceedings__

*Reg 42(2)*

☐  Declaration of solvency; members' / partners' resolutions; creditors' approval (if any).

☐  Public announcement and preliminary report.

☐  List of stakeholders; particulars of sale, realisation and distribution.

☐  Audited accounts of the liquidation.

☐  Section 59(5A) approvals (as applicable) and the Regulation 42(2) report.

__D.  PPIRP — Pre-packaged Insolvency Resolution Process__

__Form P1 — Written Consent (IRP / RP)__

*Reg 7(1)*

☐  IP registration certificate.

☐  Valid AFA and its expiry date.

__Form P2 — List of Creditors of the Corporate Debtor__

*Reg 14*

☐  Books of account and creditor ledgers.

☐  Classification of creditors (FC / OC / other; related / unrelated) with amounts and percentages.

__Form P3 — Approval of Terms of Appointment of RP__

*Reg 14(5)*

☐  Notice and attendance record of the creditors' meeting.

☐  Creditor voting record (with % of debt).

☐  The agreed fee structure of the RP.

__Form P4 — Approval for Initiating PPIRP__

*Reg 14(7)*

☐  Notice and attendance record of the creditors' meeting.

☐  The base resolution plan.

☐  Form P6 declaration by directors / partners.

☐  Members' special resolution (or partners' resolution).

☐  Creditor voting record approving initiation.

__Form P5 — Written Consent to Act as Authorised Representative__

*Reg 15(iii)*

☐  IP registration certificate and valid AFA.

__Form P6 — Declaration by Directors / Partners__

*Reg 16(1)*

☐  Board / partners' resolution.

☐  Members' special resolution approving the filing.

☐  Timeline undertaking for filing the application.

__Form P7 — Declaration Regarding Existence of Avoidance Transaction(s)__

*Reg 16(2)*

☐  Transaction analysis note (Annexure A) with supporting documents for any preferential / undervalued / extortionate / fraudulent transaction.

__Form P8 — Report of the Insolvency Professional__

*Reg 17*

☐  MSME (Udyam) registration certificate.

☐  Proof that the default is within the section 4 limit.

☐  The base resolution plan.

☐  Creditor approvals (Forms P3 and P4) and the declarations (Forms P6 and P7).

__Form P9 — Public Announcement__

*Reg 19(2)*

☐  AA order admitting the PPIRP application.

__Form P10 — List of Claims__

*Reg 20*

☐  Claim proofs received, by category.

☐  Assessment of contingent claims.

☐  Related-party determination for each creditor.

__Form P11 — Invitation for Resolution Plans__

*Reg 43*

☐  Information memorandum.

☐  Evaluation matrix.

☐  Eligibility conditions and section 29A norms.

__Form P12 — Compliance Certificate__

*Reg 49(1)*

☐  The committee-approved resolution plan.

☐  Section 29A eligibility affidavit.

☐  Committee voting record (with %).

☐  Fair value and liquidation value.

☐  Compliance table and shareholding before / after.

__Form P13 — Application for Termination of PPIRP__

*Reg 49(4)*

☐  Committee decision minutes.

☐  Report of termination of the PPIRP.

__Form P14 — Application for Vesting Management with RP__

*Reg 51*

☐  Committee meeting minutes.

☐  Reasons for vesting management with the RP (section 54J).

__E.  Personal Guarantor to Corporate Debtor__

__Form A — Written Consent to Act as Resolution Professional__

*IRP-PG Reg 4(2)*

☐  IP registration certificate and valid AFA.

☐  Confirmation of no pending disciplinary proceedings.

☐  Statement of processes in hand.

__Form B — Claim with Proof by a Creditor__

*IRP-PG Reg 7(1)*

☐  Deed of guarantee and the demand notice.

☐  Proof of debt and computation with interest up to the resolution process commencement date.

☐  Details of the related CIRP / liquidation of the corporate debtor: amount claimed, admitted and realised.

☐  Security documents (value and date), where secured.

☐  Guarantor information: assets, business, firms, bank accounts and family particulars, to the extent known.

☐  Creditor's identity / constitution proof and bank account details for transfer.

__Form C — Proxy Form for Attendance and Voting at Meeting of Creditors__

*IRP-PG Reg 16(2)*

☐  Creditor's authorisation appointing the proxy.

☐  Proof of the creditor's voting share.

☐  Identity details of the proxy holder(s).

__F.  General — for every filing__

☐  Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) / e-sign credentials for electronic filing.

☐  IBBI portal login (unique username and password) for the monitoring forms (CP / LIQ / VL / PGIRP series).

☐  Verification / notarisation completed where the form carries an affidavit.

☐  Public announcements published in the prescribed newspapers and hosted on the website, where required.

☐  An IST-dated, branded copy of the completed form retained for the case record.

☐  Confirmation of the current form version on the IBBI website before filing (forms are now revised by circular).
