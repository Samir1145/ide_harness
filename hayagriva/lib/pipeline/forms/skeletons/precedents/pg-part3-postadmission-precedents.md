# __Filing Requirements — Fees, Copies and Forms__

# __Drafting Notes__

__•	__These are template precedents for the resolution\-process stage of a personal\-guarantor matter under Chapter III of Part III\. Items A/B, D, E, F, G, H and I\-3 are filed before the NCLT; items C, I\-1 and I\-2 are RP\-issued correspondence; and I\-4 is the affected creditor’s notice\. Each is to be settled to the facts of the matter and supported by the appropriate annexures \(list of creditors, repayment plan, RP report, minutes, voting record, attendance register, return memo and fee proof\)\.

__•	__Bench and cause\-title: the default forum is the NCLT New Delhi Bench; where a CIRP or liquidation of the corporate debtor is pending, the personal\-guarantor matter is to be filed before the bench seized of that corporate process\.

__•	__Timelines to calendar: the 180\-day moratorium under Section 101; the Section 106 report timeline reckoned from the last date for submission of claims; and the not\-less\-than\-fourteen\-days notice for the meeting under Section 107\.

__•	__Approval threshold for the repayment plan: more than three\-fourths in value of creditors present and voting \(Section 111\)\.

__•	__Where no repayment plan is submitted, build the creditors’ approval into the file before filing Precedent B\. Section 106\(1A\) of the Code itself requires only that the Resolution Professional submit a report to the Adjudicating Authority; the creditors’ approval is a requirement of Regulation 17B \(to be confirmed against the substituted text\), not of the Code\.

__•	__Regulation map \(PG\-to\-CD IRP Regulations, 2019, as in the consolidated text\): list of creditors — Reg\. 9; meeting of creditors — Reg\. 11, notice contents — Reg\. 12, quorum/conduct/voting — Reg\. 13–15; contents of repayment plan — Reg\. 17 \(supervision\-and\-implementation mandate at 17\(1\)\(g\), schedule at 17\(1\)\(a\)\); meeting mandatory — Reg\. 17A; non\-submission application — Reg\. 17B; filing of the S\.106 and S\.112 reports with the AA \(within 120 days of the resolution\-process commencement date\) — Reg\. 19; breach of the repayment plan — Reg\. 20; application for discharge order — Reg\. 21; non\-cooperation by the guarantor \(RP files a statement with the AA for directions\) — Reg\. 22\. Public notice runs off Section 102 of the Code itself\.

__•	__Forms to use: by Circular No\. IBBI/IIRP/98/2026 dated 2 June 2026 \(issued under the 2019 Regulations as amended by the 2026 Amendment\), the Board specified Form A — written consent of the resolution professional \(Reg\. 4\(2\)\); Form B — claim with proof by a creditor \(Reg\. 7\(1\)\); and Form C — proxy for the meeting of creditors \(Reg\. 16\(2\)\)\. The consent, claim and proxy references in these precedents track these forms\.

__•	__Breach of the repayment plan / cheque dishonour \(Item I\) follows the Regulation 20 mechanism strictly: on forming the opinion of a failure, the RP issues the 20\(1\) notice within three days of knowledge, giving the guarantor fifteen days to address the failure or explain it; if the guarantor addresses or satisfactorily explains it, the RP reports to creditors under 20\(2\) within seven days; and only if the matter is not covered by 20\(2\) does the RP apply to the AA under Section 116\(2\) read with Regulation 20\(3\)\. A single breach does not bring the Repayment Plan to an end: Regulation 20\(3\) confers only a power to seek directions in connection with implementation, and premature end under Section 118 is a distinct consequence that arises only where the Plan is not fully implemented within the period mentioned in it\. I\-1 and I\-2 are RP correspondence; I\-3 is the Tribunal filing\. The affected creditor’s Section 138 remedy \(I\-4\) is independent of the Code and is available because the Section 101 moratorium ceases on approval of the Plan — mind the 30\-day demand and 15\-day cure timelines under the NI Act\.

__•	__Currency: these precedents reflect the IRP for PG to CD Regulations, 2019 through the 2026 Amendment \(under which the 2 June 2026 forms circular was issued\), including Regulation 17A \(mandatory meeting, 2024 Amendment\) and Regulation 17B as substituted by the 2026 Amendment — the non\-submission application now being made in accordance with Section 106\(1A\) of the Code\. Two items to confirm against the gazette before filing: \(i\) the exact text of Section 106\(1A\) and of the substituted Regulation 17B; and \(ii\) for Precedent H, the bankruptcy\-side position following the Bankruptcy \(Second Amendment\) Regulations, 2026 \(notified 1 June 2026\), which revised the bankruptcy forms and asset\-transfer coordination\.
