__Liquidation Process — Case Forms__

*Source: IBBI/LIQ/96/2026 dated 02\.06\.2026\. Claims in liquidation are filed using the corresponding CIRP claim forms \(Form B / C / D / E / F\) — references to the IRP / RP read as references to the liquidator\. Monitoring forms LIQ\-1 to LIQ\-4 \(Regulation 47B\) are filed separately on the IBBI electronic portal and are indexed in the companion deck\.*
